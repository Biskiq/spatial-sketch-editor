## World structure: supported semantics, not a persisted tree

North Star's **Build** distinguishes physical architecture, derived spatial topology and semantic spatial organization. Its destination terminology is **Space, Site, Building, Level, Zone**; an indoor **Room** is one kind of Space. Layout currently has persistent enclosed Rooms, Walls/Junctions and Wall-hosted Openings. Levels, placed structures, reusable architectural definitions/instances and validated alternatives are destination scope. Scene owns placed objects, definitions, internal components, groups, typed attachments and world presentation.

PLATE §12 uses **Site → Building → Floor → Room/Space → Content** as a future-facing orientation example. This is useful breadth evidence, not a ratified persisted containment tree. For the commission, use **Project → Building/structure → Level (floor) → Room/Space**, with **Architecture** and **Objects** as contextual organizational groupings where appropriate. “Floor” is a familiar display label and a physical architectural concept; **Level** is the destination semantic term. Do not conflate a level with a slab, or prescribe their ownership via visual nesting. Exact future schema, host expansion/isolation and slab/ceiling ownership remain open domain decisions.

Persistent hierarchy/search can be appropriate in World because creators must locate source identity, host, datum and spatial relations while editing. A Wall can be relevant to multiple Spaces and still be one Layout identity. An Opening remains hosted by its Wall. Object location in a Room context does not transfer Scene ownership. Zones may cut across containment and are not transform owners. Reusable definitions, placed instances and internal components need distinguishable source/instance reach where exposed.

**Preserve World breadth; progressively disclose World depth.** Structural context, search and selected-identity orientation are necessary breadth. Precision, specialized inspections, detailed properties and rare actions are contextual depth. World and Experience need not have equal density or identical Index contents.

## V2 accepted versus open boundary

**Strongly settled for this commission:** persistent Head with project/lens/active Experience/save-history/Preview responsibilities; stable Index, dominant continuous Stage and stable Card; contextual lower Deck; World | Experience creator axis; Scale × Depth; one current identity; owner/scope/reach; accepted material and visual direction. Index locates Presentations/relations in Experience; Card names target, owner and reach and keeps its header while lower strata collapse. Guide/extended order information has a predictable Deck home; Card does not morph into it.

Ordinary Experience is a useful 3D Stage, Index and quiet Card. A new Presentation begins with Meaning, Focus, Show and optional behavior/offer. No Guide machinery exists before a Guide; an existing Guide begins with Peek, not a full-height Deck. Camera depth progresses Auto → Hints → Capture → precise instrument. Precise Outside / Through / Plan postures share the same Camera truth; only the active grip's numeric tape is emphasized. Through remains visibly authoring, distinct from Preview.

**Compress but do not reopen:** Set is an unordered collection of reusable framings for one Presentation; coexistence implies neither sequence nor connectivity. Stops reference shared Presentations while retaining occurrence identity. A Seam is the transition into the following Stop: opening it preserves the current standpoint; route manipulation may invite a more legible Plan reading with return. Guide overview keeps abstract entry pins, rather than exposing the full Camera graph. Broader shared edits invoke Ask Rule; local edits stay quiet. Coordination is contextual depth and cannot duplicate path authoring. Multi-origin reachability must remain supportable, but its full logic is unnecessary for this shell commission. Preview's takeover and exact return remain accepted.

**Open shell work:** World hierarchy/search expression; structural breadth versus detail disclosure; World Card strata and contextual-instrument hosting; cross-lens selection/standpoint/inspection continuity; common density and responsive behavior. V2 §23 retains calibration questions about initial precise Camera posture, Deck crop-docking/spatial memory and prediction of station-bound coordination. These do not reopen the underlying Experience model. Challenge a settled treatment only for a specific cross-lens contradiction, with an explanation and a replacement preserving the product rule.
