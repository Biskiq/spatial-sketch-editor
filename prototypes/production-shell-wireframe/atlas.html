0The language

Four marks do all the work. Colour says what kind of state you are looking at; it never decorates.

Gold: selected geometryOutline and a translucent volume. Only selection is gold.
White dashes and an arrowhead: a change you can makeThe dash says “available”, the arrowhead says which way to drag.
The floor ring turns, the cross slidesPlanar by default; the ring is the handle, not a button on it.
White points: anchors that need oneWall ends, joints, a hosted item on its wall’s track. Gold when active.
Yellow: temporary inspectionSlice plane and depth volume. Never source, always Put back.
Red dashes: marked for erasingOnly the Eraser uses red.
RetiredLarge gold knob disks, XYZ gizmos, permanent W / D / H text.

1Resting workspace

The Stage is the workspace. Head, Index, Height Sheet and the dark forest dock frame it; nothing else is open.

2Dock and palettes

Arming a tool opens its icon-only palette directly above it. Picking an option or drawing keeps it open; clicking the tool again, the small chevron or Esc collapses it, and the choice is remembered.

3Object transformation

The floor ring turns, the cross slides, the small corner point scales in proportion. Clicking the point reveals width, depth and height. Values appear only on hover or while dragging. Guides keep a minimum on-screen size, so a small object at overview zoom still has room for them.

4Architecture

The same marks, but each subject shows what is geometrically meaningful for it. Hosted things move in relation to their wall.

5Roof Edit Shape

Edit Shape always opens as direct 3D editing on the roof. The 2D | 3D control becomes 3D | Section (keys 1 and 2). Both readings share one selection, one profile, one history and one Camera.

6Slice

Drawn like a Pen line: a finite, straight cut with a start and an end, a live section while drawing, a translucent yellow inspection volume and a depth ray. Flip, Face and Put back stay in the pill.

7Eraser

Immediate for simple things, one acceptance beside a bulk gesture, review only across domains. Kind filters sit behind one compact funnel, and an active filter stays visible.

8Index

Scope and facets is the Index: breadcrumbs with sibling menus, kind facets, capped lists. Collapsed, it takes no width.

9Narrow screens

The editor targets small laptops and iPad. Phones are out of scope for the editor; Preview has a Phone frame for visitors.

10One Pen, ordinary shapes

Forward compatibility only: no shape modelling is designed here. The question is how an ordinary profile fits the one Pen, the palette, the Card and the manipulation language without a second permanent tool.
