5 · Colour on a material replacement vs tint

Colour

Three outcomes the Card offers as one segmented control. Tint multiplies the image: grain and pattern stay, but it can only darken (a white-washed brick is impossible). Colour replaces the image with a flat colour and keeps relief and sheen (the S8 disabled colour map + colour literal state). A colour picked from the Paint tray is always Colour; Tint is a Card-only refinement.

6 · Card and Material editor the quick Finish vs the exact one

A Material is the raw capture — its images, real size and source — and is never edited. A Finish is a Material plus a treatment (colour, sheen, pattern) and is what a surface wears, names and saves. The Card is the Finish: four plain controls with intelligent defaults. Edit material… opens the editor beside it for exact values, the raw maps and, later, layers. Both write through one Finish adapter, so a Library draft edits exactly like a Finish on a wall.
