5 · Colour on a material replacement vs tint

Colour

Three outcomes the Card offers as one segmented control. Tint multiplies the image: grain and pattern stay, but it can only darken (a white-washed brick is impossible). Colour replaces the image with a flat colour and keeps relief and sheen (the S8 disabled colour map + colour literal state). A colour picked from the Paint tray is always Colour; Tint is a Card-only refinement.
