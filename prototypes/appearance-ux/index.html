T2.S8 U: how a creator picks, applies, edits, inspects, repairs and reuses colours and materials, built on the merged S8 engine (A–G, E7) and the Light & Sky language. Recommendation one instrument, a quick Finish and an exact one

  • A Material is not a Finish. A Material is the raw capture — its images, real size, source and licence — and is never edited. A Finish is a Material plus a treatment (colour, sheen, pattern) and is what a surface wears, names, shares and saves. A Finish is named Material · treatment (Oak Veneer · Sage stain) until someone renames it; a renamed Finish keeps its name.
  • Paint is one dock key. Arming it opens a tray above the dock: Colours and Materials tabs, favourites (★), recents, the picked item first, and one ⌃ toggle under the dropper for the full palette and Edit palette (it turns to ⌄ in the same place). The radius is Brush · Surface · Space; Surface is the default, Shift-click is a one-off Space, and Brush is a drawn placeholder for later (see the ladder).
  • Shape tells the consequence. A circle is a colour: it recolours the surface and keeps its relief and sheen (walnut stays grained). A rounded square is a material: it replaces the whole Finish. One grammar in the tray, Library and Card.
  • The Library is an asset library. A kind rail (Materials with nested categories; Objects, which Furnish opens; Openings and Plants & Lights later), sources All · Curated · This project · My library · Sources (third-party shelves later), filters, and a master–detail pane with a live preview, ★, Use for Paint, Apply and Customise…. Curated items read Veneer · 0.5 m; project and library items read from Oak Veneer · 3 changes. Every card has one anatomy (name up to two lines, one sub-line; usage and variants sit on the image), and in All the small sources are one-row shelves with See all n ›. No pin: Shift-click keeps it open for a run of picks and L reopens the last view.
  • U leaves the Index unchanged. Appearance needs no Index work: Finishes in use live in Library › This project (a click selects their users), the palette in Paint › ⌃, repair in the head. The prototype's Index is context, not a proposal; a Finish swatch per row is later, outside U.
  • The armed tool key is a quiet forest step. The armed key fills with forest 3 (#2d5e48) with an ivory icon and a faint inner edge, replacing the ivory key, so gold and cobalt stay the loudest marks on screen. It is one shell token for every tool family (World, drafting, Experience); primary buttons and pressed toggles keep their own treatment. See D12.
  • The Card is the Finish (288 px). Name (click to rename), a reach line (On North wall · only here or Shared by 6 · This wall | All 6), Material with Swap › (keeps the treatment, pattern back to real size), Colour Natural · Stained · Painted chosen by the material (wood and stone stain, plaster paints) with palette chips, Sheen Matte · Satin · Gloss with a dot on the original step, Pattern Real size, − / +, ↻ 90° and Align on wall…. Foot: ★, Save to My library, Edit material…, ⋯.
  • The Material editor is the exact Finish. About 560 px beside the Card (a bottom sheet on iPad portrait): a preview with shape, light, view and a probe, and Compare with original; Raw material map rows (read-only, each can be switched off for this Finish); Exact finish fields — a changed field's label darkens and a faint grey tick marks the original; double-click the label or slider resets that field; a Layers placeholder; N changes · Reset all · Done. It replaces the view-only inspector.
  • Library drafts write nothing. Customise… opens the Material editor on a draft (the quick Card is only for a surface clicked on the Stage): a cobalt ghost previews it on the selected surface, a click on another surface moves the preview, × or Esc cancels, and only Apply to X (one undo), Use for Paint or Save to My library commits anything. The draft edits exactly like a Finish because the Card, editor, draft and Align share one Finish adapter.
  • Two inks, one meaning each. Gold = selected (Stage outline and Index row only). Cobalt = will change (reach when All, the hover ghost, a draft). Esc, an empty click or ✕ deselects.
  • Align on wall / floor. The surface faced straight on (the Camera face intent), a metre ruler, the repeat grid, a square handle for offset and a round one for rotation; Put back exits. Overlays are never saved; every release is one undo through the same Finish writer.
  • Thumbnails are flat lit sample tiles framed on a physical window, one stored 256 px master per Finish. See U1.

Rules the design depends on

  • The Material is never edited. Raw maps are read-only in the editor; switching a map off is a Finish setting that resetting the field undoes. Swapping the Material keeps the treatment and returns the pattern to the new material’s real size.
  • Reach is visible before the edit, and defaults by kind. A curated Finish defaults to This wall (editing walnut should not silently fork every walnut surface into a project Finish); a project Finish (fin:) defaults to All n and edits in place. Cobalt outlines show who All reaches, and only then.
  • Materializing reaches one kind. All on a curated or model Finish reaches only same-kind users (F4 refuses a compound Scene + Layout registration); the Card says which users keep the original. Editing an existing fin: still reaches Layout and Scene users together.
  • A pristine clone is the curated normal form. Resetting every override returns surfaces to cat:, as E's review follow-up does.
  • Sheen shows the effective value, never rougher than captured. Roughness is a factor on the ORM map; the Card shows three steps over factor × scanned mean, disables steps rougher than the capture, marks the original's nearest step with a dot and reads Custom between steps. The editor shows the exact value.
  • No duplicated writers. The Index is hierarchy and opens the Card; the Library chooses and drafts; the Card, editor, drafts and Align write through one Finish adapter. There is no second Finish list.

Recommendation

  • Sample tile: flat, lit, with a soft sheen band across the top third so gloss and metal read, framed on a physical window (two or three boards, one brick course, a few linen threads), not a repeat count. It reads true to colour (mean colour shift ΔE00 4.7 against 46.5 for the E7 references at 192 px) with no clipping.
  • One stored master per Finish: 256 px PNG. XS (28 CSS, tighter window), S (56) and M (128, 2×) derive from it. The L detail (sphere, grazing close-up) and the Material editor preview render on demand through the E7 host, one at a time, so opening a list never fetches full-resolution images.
  • Framing hint outside the recipe: window size and centre per catalogue entry, in the same side table as thumbnails and locators. Project and imported Finishes default to a window from their tile size.
  • New thumbnail recipe version; E7's inspection recipe 2 and its 192 px references stay intact as engineering references.

The finishing ladder how later texturing joins existing seams

Progressive complexity: each step adds one idea and leaves every earlier step working, so a creator climbs only as far as the job needs. Pick → Finish → Exact are built in the prototype; Layers → Masks → Brush are agreed seams, drawn in Concepts, not designed or committed.

  • Finish layers vs Surface layers. Finish layers (Base, a Sage band, taught wear) travel with the Finish to every surface that wears it. Surface layers (strokes, decals) belong to one surface, in its own metres, and survive a Finish swap.
  • Teach turns hand strokes into a proposed layer + mask (“Wear · Near the floor · 0–35 cm”), shows in cobalt which surfaces will change, and commits only on accept. There is still one evaluator.
  • No separate workspace material model at any step: the editor, Align and a later Face / Unroll view all edit the same Finish.

Owner decisions all decided 2026-11-22

The owner accepted every recommendation below (D7: tray A). The number quick-pick (1–9) is deferred, and the quick wheel is promoted as Paint's future accelerator.

  1. Smooth and Polished concrete.

    No presentation separates them (same scan, factor only). Recommend one card, Smooth Concrete, with a Matte / Polished variant chip, and F's rule applied between cards, not between a card's variants. F's rule is then recorded between cards.

  2. Reach before the edit, replacing the post-hoc Ask.

    The plan keeps PLATE's Ask semantics. The study asks the same question earlier: a reach line on the Card (Shared by n · This wall | All n), cobalt outlines on who All reaches, defaults by kind. Nothing is asked after a drag. Recommend adopting it; it removes a dialog from every shared edit.

  3. Colour coat as new engineering.

    A colour applied with Paint keeps relief and sheen and becomes a deduplicated derived Finish (ten walls painted Sage make one Finish, following the palette). Recommend a dedicated planner over E4 primitives.

  4. G thumbnail slot to 256 px.

    Needed for a 2× M card. Recommend a scoped change: PNG, ≤ 256 KiB, edge 256, new thumbnail recipe key. Old 192 items stay valid and display with upscale until regenerated.

  5. Same-kind reach on materialize.

    All on a curated or model Finish reaches only same-kind users, with the Card naming who keeps the original. Recommend accepting as the E handoff's two-step journey; widening F4 is a separate decision.

  6. Sheen display.

    Three steps over effective roughness (factor × scanned mean), never rougher than the capture, the original marked with a dot; exact values in the editor. Recommend adopting.

  7. Tray A or mixed row B.

    Both are drawn in Concepts. Recommend A (tabs) for the clarity of consequence; B if the owner prefers fewer clicks over that clarity.

  8. Card and Material editor split.

    The Card is the quick Finish; the editor is the exact one and replaces the view-only inspector. Recommend adopting; the editor needs no new operation.

  9. Library drafts.

    Customise… opens the Material editor on a draft that writes nothing until Apply, Use for Paint or Save. Recommend adopting; it makes “try before you commit” the default way to start from the Library.

  10. Ladder seams for layers, masks and brush.

    Agree the seams now (Base layer over v1, room-fact masks, Surface layers on the decal contract, Brush inside Paint) without designing the features. Recommend recording them as the direction, not as a contract.

  11. Import reads file names first.

    The PBR map set is the industry standard, so the sheet keeps its names (Normal, Roughness, ORM) and asks only what the files leave open. Normal direction and gloss inversion come from vendor naming when present (_gl / _dx, nor_gl, arm, gloss); a clean set imports in one click (clean ↗, messy ↗). Recommend adding name detection to E5's draft facts; it never overrides an explicit answer.

  12. Armed tool key. decided 2026-11-22

    The armed key on every dock is forest 3 (#2d5e48) with an ivory icon and a 1 px inner edge at 16% white, replacing the soft ivory key. The fill alone is about 2.1 : 1 against the dock, so the icon and edge carry the state too; the tool-colour mark and the collapsed-palette caret are unchanged. U8 adopts this shared shell decision.

Before implementation what a U plan still needs

Settled 2026-11-22: D1–D12 accepted; the thumbnail treatment is recorded in the S8 plan. The rules below were accepted the same day, each chosen as the smallest rule that leaves room to grow.

  1. Stored facts.
    • Names: no flag and no format change. A Finish already stores a name; it is regenerated from Material · treatment only while it still equals the generated name, so a renamed Finish keeps its name.
    • Favourites and recents: creator preferences behind one small interface, on the device now (12 recents) and on the account later. Each entry is a reference, never a copy: a colour, cat:, a library item, or a project Finish that shows only inside its project.
    • Framing hints: an optional window size and centre per entry in the catalogue's existing presentation side table; imported and project Finishes derive theirs from tile size.
    • Thumbnail recipe: one versioned key, sample-tile/1, part of every derivative's cache key.
  2. Rules.
    • Coat identity: one coat Finish per base Finish and colour, where the colour is a palette slot or a literal. Removing a slot already turns followers into its last literal colour, so coats keep their colour.
    • Sheen: three targets on effective roughness: Matte 0.8, Satin 0.5, Gloss 0.2. A value within 0.05 of a target shows as that step, anything else as Custom; a step rougher than the capture is disabled. The targets sit in one table per material family.
    • Undo: no timer. A drag or a held arrow key previews and commits once on release; typed values commit on Enter or blur. History needs no coalescing timer.
  3. States: one rule. A refusal is a line in place with at most one action, never a dialog, and never blocks unrelated editing. Budget: a flat swatch and Too many large textures open · Retry. No world anchor: This room is disabled (Place it to see it in this room); Studio and Grazing work. Slow import: after 300 ms the summary reads Reading 3 of 5…, and Esc cancels with nothing written. My library unreachable: Offline · Retry, and Save is disabled with that reason. Empty source: one sentence and one action.
  4. From prototype to production. No new colour tokens except D12's armed key; a short table maps every prototype token to an existing presentation token. Capitalisation: sentence case for anything named or clickable, all caps only for small, non-interactive mono metadata. Future features ship no placeholders: Paint's radius shows Surface · Space until Brush exists, and the Card and editor are ordered row lists that later rows join.   • Simulated operations and history; no file IO (imports use stand-in texture sets), no network, no real Camera.

  • No creator study. The four tasks were walked by the author, not by creators.
  • Coarse pointer designed but not checked on an iPad; the board's iframes cannot emulate one.
