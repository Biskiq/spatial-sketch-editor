**1. Executive verdict**

**Research 6 reinforces Biskiq’s North Star; it does not justify replacing the architecture or restructuring T1–T4. It does justify a small priority correction and stronger evidence for creative continuity.**

Multiple starting points, optional Guides, explicit relationships, truthful ingest, typed resources, revision diagnostics and shared execution are already unusually specific ratified commitments—not merely broad language that could accommodate the research. The strongest contribution is making them testable through concrete creator journeys. The weakest part is treating a large collection of preferred mechanisms as foundational guarantees or “planned extensions.” Research cannot confer either status.

Three findings matter most:

- **Guide-free authoring has a real delivery gap.** The accepted source format allows zero Guides, but the new lowering currently selects Presentations only through Stops. An unguided Experience can retain Presentations in source while dropping them from its lowered program. This is a bounded capability omission, not evidence that Experience needs a different authority.
- **Revision confidence needs more than successful reference resolution.** Research 6 usefully separates technical survival from compositional fitness. Existing contracts already require impact reporting; the missing work is concrete domain checks, explainable repair and usable presentation—not a second dependency system.
- **The first-day journey is still over-equipped.** Ten photographs, a model, wall mounting, persistent rails, captions, illumination and publishing span several unbuilt capabilities. Rails, sophisticated lighting and procedural ceilings should not become admission requirements for a beginner’s successful result.

Keep **Core Editor Stability** as the near-term target without adding publication, persistent attachments, general revision auditing or advanced scenography. Complete the useful editor loop, then prioritize meaningful guide-free Experiences ahead of richer choreography. “A continuing creative project, from a simple result to deliberate refinement” is a stronger general product framing than making scenographic specialization the product’s identity. Scenography remains a valuable creative vocabulary and set of recipes.

**4. Architecture assessment: what actually needs work**

**A. Guide-free execution is the clearest concrete omission.** In [unit types](../packages/project-model/src/units/types.ts), `Experience.entryGuideId` may be null and `guides`/`stops` may be empty. In [lowering](../packages/project-model/src/units/experience/lowering.ts), `usedPresentationIds` is built from ordered Stops. [The companion](../packages/project-model/src/units/experience/companion.ts) also enumerates destinations from program Stops; [program actions](../packages/project-model/src/program/types.ts) navigate to `stopId`.

A read-only probe using the canonical fixture writer produced: **valid source, two Presentations, zero Guides/Stops → successful lowering, zero program Presentations, null entry Stop**. Thus “zero Stops accepted” cannot be claimed as a complete unguided Experience. Fixing this should establish explicit non-Guide entry/activation and accessible content discovery through the same interpreter and Camera authority. Do not manufacture invisible Stops to satisfy the existing path. Basic navigation may initially be deliberately bounded; unrestricted walking and collision/navigation geometry need their own proof.

**B. Resolve authored inputs before treating the prepared runtime as a live product.** [S06 acceptance residuals](../docs/roadmap/t3-experience/t3.p01-camera-experience-foundation/t3.p01.s06-experience-runtime-and-camera-handoff/acceptance.md#residuals) explicitly say that text, invocation selection, state, lifecycle and channel policy are closed-fixture inputs without an authored F2 home. The source Presentation type also has no subject focus binding. These are existing activation/authoring obligations, made more consequential by Research 6; a host must not fill the holes with another editable configuration store. Exact supported source/program amendments belong to the first authorized consumer. A complete clip, UI or interaction language is unnecessary for that step.

**C. Revision triage needs bounded semantics, not a universal solver.** Use three independent questions: does the reference resolve; does its declared relationship still hold; is the selected program executable? “Needs Review” can describe changed framing despite intact identity. “Preserved” must mean that the checks for a declared relationship passed—not that the system understands all artistic intent. A fixed captured View should stay fixed when its subject moves unless tracking was explicitly authored.

Research’s non-blocking edit principle is sound **only where validators admit a coherent repairable state**. It cannot override F4 atomicity, invalid topology or a required resource-lock invariant. Nor may a repair erase an overflowing work, select an arbitrary replacement face or discard an Experience. Release blockage depends on the selected program/profile’s requirements; accepting a warning cannot waive executable failure.

For first implementation, report affected uses from existing qualified references and domain correspondence, with conservative invalidation. The [ratification’s dependency rules](../docs/reference/decisions/northstar-ratification-2026-09-27.md#dependencies-invalidation-and-deterministic-evaluation) already prescribe this and permit coarse units. No new graph database, global constraint engine or “revision service” is justified. Persistent surface anchors will still need genuine domain mapping policies; F1’s runtime-only [`DerivedSourceRef`](../packages/project-model/src/references/types.ts) is not a durable face attachment. Similarly, [`planMoveHorizontal`](../packages/project-model/src/scene-planning/operations.ts) preserves Y and checks support for that operation; it establishes no continuing host relationship after a Layout revision.

**D. Correct four architectural overclaims before adopting research proposals.**

- **Frames and Rooms:** world-local roots remain the default; optional owner-qualified frames need actual owners. Rooms are persistent semantic regions reconciled against derived faces, not disposable mesh identities. There is no mandatory room origin or containment-based transfer.
- **Representation:** Camera owns viewing/projection; Layout owns cut/depth/peel/reveal evaluation and architectural correspondence. The shell composes temporary inspection parameters. Do not move all clipping meaning into Camera.
- **Reuse:** Scene declares intrinsic object capabilities and bindings; portable states/clips/performances retain typed-resource identity and revision. Experience owns contextual uses, not an independent state engine.
- **Rehearsal:** idempotent target evaluation cannot reconstruct unrecorded visitor choices. [F’s session semantics](../docs/reference/composition-execution.md#session-state-and-declarative-logic) require explicit initial state plus events/checkpoints for a known run. Preview shortcuts must disclose a chosen scenario and suppress inappropriate external effects.

The research’s dual-skin shell, straight-skeleton construction, transcript-span schema, DOM pool sizes, texture compression and physics hooks remain mechanisms or future capabilities. Preserving extension points does not promise migration-free support for every future behavior.
