# Vertical and overhead architecture — RR-1 / RR-2 research commission

Status: decision input, 2026-10-21. Planning/research only; no ratification, dependency adoption, implementation authorization or production-format change. Owner acceptance supplied for this commission: P3's intuitive expressive architectural sketching, P2's intelligent spatial revision and P1's optional Room/Guide/Stop starting point; P4 remains deferred.

## Decision and first consumer

Decide which canonical Layout model can preserve P3's expressive drawing and trustworthy revision across irregular elevations, multiple structures, partial floors and independent interior/exterior overheads. Select a first native family and a credible expansion path, rather than freezing the prototype schema or treating flat caps as the product ceiling.

Proposed first consumer: sketch two unequal-height pavilion wings, add a partial mezzanine and bridge without creating a Room, cover multiple enclosed regions with one editable roof, keep a calm suspended ceiling beneath it, then cut a lightwell through selected overhead/floor elements. Revise one datum, one Wall and one roof section; inspect in Plan/Section/3D. P2 must be able to query the resulting canonical support surfaces. A Guide, Stop, retained building generator and visitor representation capture are unnecessary for this first geometry decision.

The packet must answer: what moves when a creator raises a structure, a level, a slab or an eave; what keeps identity; what physically joins; what remains independent; and what refuses. Importing the same pavilion is the comparison path, especially for forms outside supported native families.

## Initial findings: what is settled, and what is missing

**Authority.** Layout owns levels, placed structures, architectural definitions/instances, alternatives and validity through one canonical architectural compiler. Project-world placement permits explicit internal frames; Rooms are optional derived semantic regions, not mandatory containers or coordinate systems. A drawing gesture can create Walls, a slab or canopy without changing that ownership. Neither a roof engine nor a general mesh layer may become a second architectural authority; using a library inside the compiler does not itself create a second authority.

**Authority.** Reference identity includes revision, stable instance path and Layout location before vertical expansion. Generated fragments carry source correspondence and generation, never durable triangle identity. Representation consumes canonical output in runtime-safe code; Camera owns projection/viewing. One accepted operation uses expected revision, coherent validation and one Undo result. Inspection sheet height is session context; capturing representation intent is a separate F.3/Experience concern.

**Observed production.** [`LayoutDocumentWallFirst` and `LayoutWall`](../../../packages/layout-core/src/layout-wall-first-types.ts) provide one Floor datum, scalar per-Wall positive height with no global upper bound, and physical Walls shared between Rooms. [`compile` geometry](../../../packages/layout-core/src/layout-geometry.ts) derives Room ceiling elevation as Floor elevation plus maximum boundary-Wall height. This is current format 5, not a multi-level or roof contract. Vertical richness needs more than increasing a height limit.

**Accepted planning.** Independent Layout-owned CeilingRegions carry footprint, height/profile, thickness and closure/suspended intent. Room closure remains generated only where uncovered; suspended ceilings do not erase enclosure. Ambiguous authored closure overlaps refuse pending a joint rule. D3 prefers explicit one-shot fits with previews, deferring persistent following. D4 requires one authoritative Wall-top definition and explicit compatibility policy. These commitments narrow the inquiry without settling its schema.

**Missing commitments:** durable level identity versus numeric datum; structure-frame composition and cross-structure connection; host topology expansion versus isolated structures; split-level shared-Wall segmentation; slab/roof/ceiling family distinctions; thickness convention; closure coverage and intentional gaps; void target scope; support versus following; correspondence through roof split/merge; supported surface domain and tolerance policy. Proposed types below are not ratified names.

**Observed P3 strengths.** [`types.ts`](../../../prototypes/p3-vellum/src/model/types.ts) separates roof `Overhead`, `Deck`, `Void`, posts and wall `Run`; free canopy and deck creation do not require enclosure. [`roofJoin.ts`](../../../prototypes/p3-vellum/src/model/roofJoin.ts) narrowly qualifies joins and previews the settled result before deleting the absorbed source. `coverCheck` and `ohHasSurface` exemplify one qualification answer for offers, edits and geometry. Preserve these behavioral properties without porting the storage model.

**Observed P3 limits.** `Run.baseY`, `RoomSeed.level/floorY/height` and `Overhead.baseY` are numeric heights; [`settle.ts:levelArrangements`](../../../prototypes/p3-vellum/src/model/settle.ts) bins Walls by 0.05 m height, not durable Level ID. Distinct co-height structures can share a band; nearly equal heights can cross bins. `carryStanding` infers affected parts by height/proximity. Roof `restsOn`/`fit.rooms` and [`derive.ts:overheadForWall`](../../../prototypes/p3-vellum/src/model/derive.ts) couple behavior to Rooms/coverage. These shortcuts cannot establish F.1 frames, authored support or following. P3 itself says moving a building across storeys needs real levels.

**Inference.** Preserve the sheet as an intuitive working-plane/context instrument with explicit world height and location qualification. A creator can discover or create a datum from a gesture; no mandatory global storey wizard follows. A Room-based shortcut may seed ordinary elements, but those elements must remain independently addressable after the Room divides, disappears or never exists.

## Geometry harvest: carry forward, qualify, and challenge

1. **Reported, not newly verified:** library sizes, timings, noise/sliver outcomes, skeleton comparisons and “0.000 m” mesh error came from the harvest's scratch run/audit. Its scratch script is outside the repository; exact environment and assertions are not fully reproducible from this note. They justify focused probes, not acceptance thresholds or a dependency choice. HEAD now contains reusable [`tests/support/roofProbe.ts`](../../../prototypes/p3-vellum/tests/support/roofProbe.ts), so the harvest's “uncommitted audit probe” provenance is historical, not the present inventory.
2. **Observed:** wing decomposition is bounded: near-axis-aligned outline, at most 16 vertices/four wings, local epsilon/merge rules and restricted sections. `buildCompound` uses planar cells plus polygon difference/intersection. Curves are sampled; “exact valleys” means agreement with the chosen piecewise-planar model, subject to floating-point clipping. It proves neither analytic curved-surface accuracy nor topology for arbitrary footprints.
3. **Observed:** `probeRoof` compares the top mesh with its own `ohH` evaluator, skips points within 5 cm of the boundary and uses strict interior triangle hits. It is a valuable consistency oracle but shares assumptions and misses seams, thin slivers, boundary cracks, undersides and vertical faces. Add independent geometry and edge-incidence checks; do not call a finite sample grid a proof of manifoldness.
4. **Unresolved:** the harvest itself flags its equal-pitch hip equivalence to Chebyshev distance as unverified for P3's greedy cover/hip choices (§7 S-B). Verify over concave/holey cases and transformed frames before adopting that oracle. Do not generalize equal-eave orthogonal wavefront claims to arbitrary weighted, unequal-eave or curved roofs.
5. **Observed:** P3 `ohTopAt = ohUndersideAt + thick` uses vertical thickness, not normal thickness. A steep roof and a suspended flat ceiling therefore need an explicit contract; a roof-following ceiling is not inherently a skeleton problem. Vertical offset is simple, planar normal offsets require joint/intersection handling, and a general shell offset is a materially larger family.
6. **External correction:** CGAL's weighted skeleton constructors require positive weights, but [`extrude_skeleton`](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Straight_skeleton_2/group__PkgStraightSkeleton2Extrusion.html) explicitly permits zero weight for a vertical edge. The harvest's categorical gable limitation is too broad. Wrapper exposure is still unverified. The same API warns that maximum-height results can have non-local self-intersections: combinatorial 2-manifold output is not sufficient geometric validity.
7. **External qualification:** [CGAL's manual](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Straight_skeleton_2/index.html) describes straight-line polygon wavefronts and nonintersecting hole contours; that does not solve vaults, arbitrary profile edits or semantic support. [Clipper2's official overview](https://www.angusj.com/clipper2/Docs/Overview.htm) describes integer internal clipping, coordinate-range limits and scaling. That is not evidence that the independent TS port has identical arithmetic guarantees. Precision, normalization and port conformance remain probes.
8. **External qualification:** [Manifold](https://github.com/elalish/manifold#manifold-library) requires manifold mesh input and reports errors for incompatible imports. A valid mesh Boolean supplies neither native Wall/Level semantics nor durable correspondence. Its blanket reliability wording must not become a claim that arbitrary imported architecture is semantically editable.
9. **Inference:** upper envelopes are useful for an explicitly joined single-valued roof family. They must not silently replace overlapping independent roofs, stacked decks or a bridge over a canopy with only their maximum height. Different eaves/families can introduce discontinuities, fascias and cavities; “use an envelope” alone does not resolve those joins. Keep area overlap distinct from boundary adjacency and from declared joining.

## Alternatives to compare

| Alternative | Creative ceiling and strengths | Cost, failure mode and disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Global ordered storeys, elements owned by one storey | Familiar conventional building edits; cheap Plan filtering and repeated floors | Atriums, sloping/overlapping floors, split levels and bridges become exceptions; global ordering confuses unrelated structures. Keep as an optional view/preset over richer datums, not the universal source. |
| B. Explicit local datums + structure/instance frames + independently identified architectural elements | Irregular/coincident levels, open platforms, repeated pavilions and whole-structure transforms; keeps wall-first gestures and F.1 qualification | Requires explicit shared-Wall segmentation and cross-frame joining rules. Best provisional vertical core; level is an identity/context, not necessarily a bounded volume or mandatory parent. |
| C. Per-element elevations without durable levels, as P3 approximates | Immediate drawing anywhere; no global stack | Co-height is not identity; datum revisions need proximity heuristics and references lose context. Useful UI freedom, insufficient canonical model alone. |
| D. Mass/Space-owned cells generating Walls/floors/roof | Whole-building push/pull and conventional envelope regeneration are direct | Makes free/curved Walls and no-Room canopies secondary; conflicts with derived-Room ownership if canonical. Compare only a retained Layout definition/generator that emits canonical elements with explicit correspondence/detach policy, not Room ownership. |
| E. Independent semantic surface families over bounded analytic/profile patches and height envelopes | Flat/shed/gable, radial cone/dome/vault families, multi-Room roofs, slabs, suspended ceilings; parameters survive intuitive edits | A single height function cannot represent folded-over roofs or stacked surfaces. Use multiple identified patches/families and declared seams, holes, joins and support relations. Recommended overhead direction; do not require every family to share one solver. |
| F. Universal dual-skin shell/B-rep/CSG/SDF source | Rich overhangs, undercuts, dormers, cavities and general intersections | Expensive validity, offset, topology naming and revision semantics; loses the clean gestures unless semantic features remain primary. Kernel use inside Layout is admissible; general authored mesh/CSG/SDF editing remains outside the active program. Reopen a bounded feature only on a failed accepted task. |
| G. Skeleton-driven polygonal roof family; detailed architecture imported | Broader polygonal hips/courtyards; imports cover ornate/freeform exceptions | Skeleton is a family implementation, not all roofs; initialization/licensing/port questions remain. Import preserves visual richness but limits native revision and semantic supports. Keep as a measured expansion/comparison, not a universal replacement. |

These axes can combine: B+E can use a skeleton kernel for one polygonal hip family and import another pavilion. A retained parametric definition must have one source of truth: either parameters with explicit generated-element correspondence/overrides, or detached canonical elements. Persisting both as independent writers is not an alternative.

## Provisional recommendation and falsifiers

Commission B+E with explicit one-shot fitting first. Levels are named datums in qualified frames; explicit element offsets preserve arbitrary heights. Structure frames govern placement/reuse, not Room containment. Keep a conventional storey workflow as a compound gesture over ordinary elements. Compare two compiler organizations: expand structure instances into one qualified host topology, versus compile isolated local structures with explicit declared connection boundaries; neither may union by overlap. Do not freeze this choice before the bridge/shared-Wall probe.

Separate semantic roles: a roof carries exterior overhead form; a ceiling carries independent interior closure/suspension; a slab carries floor/deck geometry; a void identifies an intentional cut; supports identify geometric relations. Shared boundary/profile evaluation is desirable, but a common “surface” base type must not erase those meanings. Scene owns presentation/material configuration over supported identities; Layout owns physical geometry and closure. Support diagnostics are geometric, not structural-engineering certification.

Each Wall has one authoritative base/top specification, with roof-fit as an explicit supported variant or an accepted bake, never a competing height writer. Refuse cycles such as roof eave follows Wall top while Wall top follows that roof. Resolve whether base discontinuities split physical Wall identity or produce explicitly addressed vertical segments; do not infer topology from nearest elevation.

For voids compare explicit host references plus a local cut volume against a region swept through a qualified height interval with an explicit target set. Prefer explicit target scope provisionally: a lightwell should not cut an unrelated bridge merely because it overlaps in XZ. Decide whether a void remains authored but unresolved after host removal, and whether movement follows hosts or remains frame-fixed. Cutting is not deleting the underlying footprint.

Falsifiers: ordinary accepted revisions repeatedly need automatic following to remain understandable; recognized building handles vanish during common edits; split-level shared Walls cannot keep coherent source identity without arbitrary fragments; canopy/ceiling/skylight tasks require overfolded geometry beyond patch families; or imports satisfy the same revision tasks with materially less authoring burden. Report the exact failed behavior and compare retained definitions, declared following or one additional bounded family. Do not silently downgrade owner-accepted sketching to flat roofs or change derived-Room authority.

## Focused probes — executable next work, not performed here

Its README must provide install/run commands and emit machine-readable pass/refuse diagnostics plus SVG/mesh/Section artifacts. Reuse harvest fixtures/oracles instead of another library survey. No existing scratch timing is a pass criterion.

Failure/refusal vocabulary is a probe deliverable, not new F type names: invalid input; unsupported family; ambiguous join/closure; unresolved/removed reference; cyclic dependency; stale revision/evaluation; kernel failure. Geometrically valid unsupported support/headroom can be an explicit warning only where the proposed domain validity permits it. P3's “loose ends never block” is not a production-wide rule. A failed commit preserves prior source; a failed drag can retain last valid preview with a reason. Never substitute first polygon, empty intersection or hidden fallback geometry for a kernel failure.

## Deliverables, decision gates and ownership

**Execution bound:** first decide datum/top/closure semantics with V1, V2 and O1 fixtures; these can be hand-constructed data/evaluator probes, not a new editor. Reproduce G1 and R1 only for the selected initial geometry family. O2 opens for a selected void/support operation; G2 probes only the concrete family limit that could change that decision. Other expansion cases receive a documented refusal/defer boundary and trigger, not mandatory implementation. Stop once the first consumer is defensibly specified; do not build all alternatives to compare their semantics.

Deliver: (1) decision table for datums/frames, structure topology and Wall segmentation; (2) overhead/closure/void/support ownership diagram and validity matrix; (3) reproducible fixture harness with independent oracles, counterexamples and measured approximation limits; (4) identity/correspondence tables through revision and reload; (5) native/import task comparison; (6) smallest first-consumer interface/codec delta and a bounded expansion list. All artifacts distinguish passed, refused, failed and untested. Ship no general framework from a successful spike.

Pass to production planning only when the selected first-consumer script has coherent frame conversion, source identity, deterministic qualification, honest closure/cut geometry, and revision/Undo/reload behavior; all adversarial inputs either pass declared invariants or give typed refusals; no silent fallback or second evaluator exists. Define numerical tolerances before execution with units, local coordinate range and analytic-vs-faceted error budgets. Historical `1e-9` bars and sampled zeros are proposals to review, not inherited product guarantees. Performance is reported against a named environment and consumer budget chosen for that plan.

Stop and return a bounded decision if a common accepted task fails; a dependency cannot be reproducibly tested; persistent fit requires an unapproved identity/correspondence policy; shared-Wall or closure ambiguity cannot be explained; or the model needs general mesh editing. A failed family can be refused/imported without invalidating the whole direction, provided that does not remove an owner-accepted ordinary task. No production authorization follows automatically from passing research.

Proposed lead: T1/Layout for RR-1/RR-2, canonical compiler, representation and validity. T2/Scene supplies the first support-query consumer and attachment/correspondence requirements; it must not reconstruct roofs from render meshes. Camera reviews frame/projection consumption; T3 joins only for a concrete representation-capture/invocation consumer. P3 supplies creator journeys; owner judges fidelity to accepted sketching and any changed design promise. P4 is not a prerequisite.

Persisted datum/frame/top/surface changes require explicit Layout codec/version or repository-approved fresh-authority cutover; preserve current format-5 descriptions until landing. Reuse F.1/AM-1 reference and frame shapes, F.2 units and F.4 acceptance. Draft/ratify an F amendment only for a genuinely absent cross-domain reference/channel/acceptance shape; geometric family growth within existing guarantees is domain planning. Unsupported required families reject explicitly. New representation parameters declare F.3 semantics, units, ranges, arbitration and invalidation; no editor recipe becomes a visitor format. Register consuming production work/gates separately, and route any shell departure through PLATE. This commission changes none of those authorities.
