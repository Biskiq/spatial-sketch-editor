Judge contrast here first: Paper, a pale Wall, the gold selection and a dark material.

Draw ink, snap cue, refusal

One black line, a black end mark, one length tag. The end mark's shape is the snap cue: ● free · ○ on a Wall or end · □ on the grid. Swing a segment across the Wall: placed segments stay; only the live one turns red dashed.

Click to place · double-click or Enter to finish · click the first dot to close a Room · Backspace removes a point · Esc cancels.

Drag preview ghost of a Wall edit (2D)

The source stays put. The proposal is one dashed centreline per changed Wall — the same line you drew, dashed in the ghost colour. Rooms and objects use the same dashed language in Transform ghosts. Drag an end knob or the two-way arrow.

Drag ● to move a junction (the neighbour follows) · drag ⟷ to slide the Wall · Esc while dragging cancels.

Object gizmo move, turn, W·D·H (3D)

No ring: one bent two-way arrow beside the object turns it, and travels with it as it turns. Every gesture shows the object's dashed edges at the proposed pose in the ghost colour; the object itself stays put until release. The chip does two things: click shows the W·D·H arrows and the height stem, drag scales the whole object uniformly. The stem has two knobs: the top one moves the top, the bottom one lifts the object off its support (objects only). Values are deltas from where the gesture began.

Drag the bent arrow to turn (15° steps, hold Alt for free) · drag ✥ to move · drag the chip to scale · click the chip, then drag the face arrows or either ⬢ · Esc cancels.

Wall handles 3D

End knobs change length (the joined Wall follows). The stem beyond the free end has the top knob only: Walls cannot be lifted (no Wall Base yet). The arrow on the cap sets thickness, the arrow on the face slides the Wall. No black disc under any arrow.

Length and slide show the one dashed centreline; top and lift show the dashed centre plane; thickness shows the two dashed faces on the cap.

Ruler measure and scale

Ink on Paper in both readings (black drawing ink). The ends use the same snap shapes as drawing.

Drag anywhere to measure · Esc clears.

Gear Room labels and Paper, per view

The gear holds view preferences for this viewer, never project data. Room labels: 2D on, 3D off by default. Paper is picked per view from three Paper tones, so the 2D plan and the 3D board can mix and match. Each Paper row opens up into a swatch tray inside the panel (the panel grows) instead of a dropdown. Double-click a 2D label to rename it in place (optional; the Card title is the primary rename).

Transform ghosts one language for Walls, Rooms and objects (2D shown; 3D draws the same lines)

While a gesture is live, the subject stays where it is and its proposal is drawn dashed in the ghost colour; releasing commits it as one Undo step, Esc drops it. Walls and Rooms show their dashed centrelines. Objects show their dashed outline: the footprint in 2D, the box edges in 3D in Object gizmo. A turn shows the same outline at the new angle. A refusal turns the ghost red dashed with its reason. Moving a Room moves its connected group: Rooms that share a Wall travel together, because they cannot be pulled apart without detaching architecture.

Drag ✥ on a Room to move its group · drag ✥ on an object (or its body) to move it · drag its bent arrow to turn it (15° steps, Alt for free) · drag the Wall's ⟷ to slide it · drop anywhere to commit · drag a Room onto the free Wall to see a refusal.

A Room and its connected Walls move as a Layout group. Scene objects keep their world placement and stay put when the Room moves.
