## Product direction for T5

The architecture G0 recommends must serve this direction:

- Biskiq supports **effortless semantic and geometric editing in World**, alongside a **dedicated
  Object Focus workspace** for deeper, more precise and expressive 3D modeling.
- Both share the **same authored source, identity, relationships and history**. Object Focus is not
  a second document, scene, format, history or acceptance path.
- The **drawing-first philosophy and its progressive learning curve are preserved**, without
  limiting the eventual geometric capabilities to what the existing prototypes demonstrate.
- The final **Object Focus UX and technical architecture remain open** for G0 and subsequent design.
- T5 investigates a sophisticated, extensible geometric foundation, **reusing established
  mathematical libraries and algorithms where appropriate**. This record prescribes no kernel,
  language, operation set, representation or feature sequence.
