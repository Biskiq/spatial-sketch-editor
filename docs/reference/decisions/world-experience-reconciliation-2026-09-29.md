# World | Experience reconciliation amendment

**Date:** September 29, 2026. **Status:** owner-directed amendment to the
[September 27 north-star ratification](./northstar-ratification-2026-09-27.md),
following the [product model](../../World-Experience-Model.md),
[architecture synthesis](../../World-Experience-Architecture-Synthesis.md), and
reconciliation with current source and contracts. The September 27 record remains
the historical ratification and normative baseline except where this later
amendment refines it. This record supplies decision provenance; the routed live
contracts below own the current destination statements. Nothing here declares a
production cutover.

| Amended concern | Accepted direction and live owner |
| --- | --- |
| Product framing | **World \| Experience** are creator-facing lenses over one project, not merged semantic documents. The replacement shell's composition remains open. [North Star](../north-star.md) owns product direction; [PLATE](../design-system/editor-shell-and-visual-system.md) owns shell and visual constraints. |
| Visitor meaning and identity | The September 27 authored **Destination** concept is now **Presentation**: reusable, Experience-owned visitor-facing composition. A **Stop** is a stable Guide occurrence referencing a Presentation; each session visit/invocation has its own runtime identity. “Destination” remains ordinary target/address vocabulary, not a second authored entity. A Presentation is distinct from a reusable performance resource. [North Star](../north-star.md) and [F.1–F.2](../composition-execution.md) own these contracts. |
| Camera and Experience | One Camera authority owns Views, routes, framing/projection and movement evaluation. Experience owns Presentations, Stops, Guide order, continuation, Gates and Camera invocation context. Cut invents no spatial edge; Travel needs a supported route or reports a gap. Legacy Camera-node order and Guide order cannot remain two writable authorities. [Camera](../components/camera-tour.md) owns domain behavior; [F.2](../composition-execution.md) owns the unit boundary. |
| Execution and repair | Exclusive channel conflicts reject by default; only declared replacement/handoff on supporting channel families may supersede a run, and stale runs cannot reclaim or clear a newer contribution. Explicit unresolved authored bindings can remain repairable; Preview refuses or disables affected required behavior, and publication requires executable closure. [F.1, F.3–F.5](../composition-execution.md) own these rules. |

Current Scene | Camera shell behavior and Scene-encoded Camera data remain landed
descriptions until explicit migration. The interior-View-to-Stop meaning and
detour lifecycle were carried into Prototype V2 design as hypotheses; the
accepted direction is now the [final V2 synthesis](../../../prototypes/integrated-experience-authoring/design/Prototype-V2-final-synthesis.md),
and the next step is repo-aware reconciliation and implementation planning.
