## Reconciliation and limits

The North Star's blanket treatment of sculpting/general geometry as external-tool territory and
its architectural-only reading of native geometric depth are superseded by the evidence-led
admission boundary. A technique is neither forbidden nor promised solely by its representation.
Ordinary work must remain approachable; specialized depth must justify its creative value and cost.
The product's permanent non-goals and the value of external-tool exchange remain.

The reconciled proposal supersedes the two original proposals as the foundation for this ruling,
but is **not ratified wholesale**. D2–D5's concrete operation/order/edit-contract choices, directed
hybrid strategy, all proposed prototypes, guide/band/arrangement fixtures, participant counts,
thresholds, performance numbers and R1–R5 milestones remain historical recommendations. No
specific implementation direction, geometry representation, kernel, gesture grammar, milestone,
slice count or sequence is selected. Contextual creation and progressive depth are destination
principles; claims of interaction transfer, learnability or competitive advantage still need evidence.

Source proposals A/B, the kernel assessment and the reconciliation remain at their original paths
as historical evidence, with supersession pointers. The original G0 commission is retained as its
historical brief; its request for three to five implementation-ready increments does not bind future
planning. Existing landed behavior, accepted gate identities, closed phases, named consumer decisions,
PLATE shell authority, F contracts and production release discipline are unchanged. This ruling
creates no production capability or scope obligation in another track.

## Next authorization

The owner must separately authorize a **bounded E0 experimental commission** before any experiment
or prototype starts. It must identify the first creative job and alternatives, fair comparison,
minimum semantic/numerical evidence, decision criteria, cost envelope and stop condition, with the
review path and authorized work explicit. The prototype design in the proposal is one candidate,
not that commission. The [T5 decision questions](../../roadmap/t5-expressive-geometry/README.md#e0-next-experimental-decision-gate)
remain open product decisions rather than implied approvals.

E0 must return an accept/revise/reject decision on a bounded next investment; failure may simplify,
defer or redirect T5. Any later production work still needs owning-track review, consumer-proven
F amendments where required, dependency/licensing decisions, registered phase and slice plans and
an explicit owner release. No prototype, dependency, production implementation or F amendment is
authorized here. Merging this documentation would not authorize E0 or production.
