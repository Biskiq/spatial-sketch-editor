## 3. Material contradictions and their resolution

1. **Status lag.** P25's old UX-lock paragraph says #114 has not started;
   roadmap step 7 says UX2 is next. `current.md` and the old plan still carry UX6's
   checkpoint/unrun account. The final UX4–UX6 follow-up in the UX evidence record
   supersedes several WIP bullets: Node 148/148 and a serial 26-axis run are recorded,
   the C9 strip is retired, and ten UX456 runtime captures exist. These are historical
   readings at their recorded revision, not new verification or owner UX acceptance.
   UX7 was not started; V3 replaces its pending work direction.
2. **Card identity versus task reading.** PLATE calls the edge/point/Cue Card a
   canonical selected-entity inspector. The revised old plan §3.1 instead keeps
   `S.sel` on the from-Stop while the Card shows edge, point or Cue session readings.
   V3 must declare selection and task context separately and make the persistent
   Card identity honest. A title change alone cannot reconcile this mismatch.
3. **Spatial identity versus schematic constellation.** PLATE and the instrument
   grammar explicitly compress View distance and deny metric meaning; their Guide
   posture uses Stop-entry pins. That was a declared schematic design, not actual
   Camera placement. V3 recommends actual View positions for spatial authoring and
   selected-Stop Views for occurrence work. It must replace those scoped rules and
   solve co-located access without inventing Stop geometry or moving Camera truth.
4. **Exposure preservation versus deliberate prototype pruning.** The old plan
   promises every accepted C9 capability remains reachable and does not reopen
   product decisions. V3 intentionally defers Cue/action/Gate/detour authoring and
   proposes new Stop-local sequences. Preserve valid authored capabilities and
   compatibility; stop treating every old writer as mandatory visible demo UI.
5. **Deck breadth versus useful spatial work.** PLATE makes Guide, Path topology
   and Journey canonical broad lower lanes. The topology lane repeats Stage points;
   a Journey-first station editor demands timing knowledge before useful playback.
   V3 recommends contextual Path controls and compact playback, with broader time
   editing earned by information rather than inherited from a posture name.
6. **Accepted capability versus accepted design.** C9 MP1–MP4 acceptance, test
   counts, Presenter completion and successful surface assertions remain evidence
   of their tested concerns. They do not establish visual/product conformance.
   New board criteria judge the proposed creator workflow and require owner review.

## 6. Invariants retained and review boundary

- Camera remains the single owner of Views, framing, routes, projection and movement
  evaluation. Cut authors no connection; Travel uses explicitly supported direct
  paths or reports a gap. Guide order never authorizes shortest-path graph routing.
- Experience owns reusable Presentations, stable Stop occurrences, editorial order,
  continuation and holds. Layout/Scene/Camera/Experience remain distinct authorities.
  Proposed Stop-local View order does not order the Presentation inventory or edit
  its shared Default; exact model/runtime changes require ratification.
- Authored interior anchors stay separate from generated endpoints and samples.
  Selection, playheads, task/navigation return and rendered images remain session or
  derived state. Navigation and Back preserve accepted edits; they are not Undo.
- One source-history authority, explicit shared-edit reach, repairable stable
  references, neutral lens/layout changes and isolated visitor execution remain.
  Production F formats, World A–F and Paper adoption are not changed by this package.
