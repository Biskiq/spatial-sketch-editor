T1 next batch: UX proposals

Eleven proposals for the questions that decide the most. 1–6 settle a model or a gesture in one figure; 7–11 are step-by-step interaction designs for Spaces, Interior Reveal, Slice and the rotunda. They use the existing PLATE vocabulary: black ink for built Walls, cobalt dashes for a proposal, red dashes for a refusal, disc knobs for points, gold for the active handle, dark mono tags for live values, yellow for temporary inspection, the Card's scoped list and the Stage state pill. Static and untested with creators. Part of the T1 next-batch proposal, revision 3.

1. Split and join: where does a joint come from, and how does it go away?

Question: can Junction editing ship without a new tool, a new mark or an armed Card action?

Recommended. No Junction tool and no selectable Junction subject. Split from the scoped list; join by the existing removal gesture and the Eraser. The one grammar edit is a sentence in the Wall row extending bend-point removal to a two-Wall joint.

2. Duplicate a Room that shares a Wall

Question: what happens to the shared boundary and the door through it?

Recommended. One meaning for Duplicate regardless of neighbours. The isolated case ships first on the existing planner; the shared case adds a copy-enclosure planner, not topology surgery.
Decided: the copy starts clear of its source. A free island resting on another group's Wall reads overlaps, not joined on the coincident stretch. Weld is outside this batch.

3. Connecting to a circle: three models

Question: which of these is “a simple connection”, and which is a new architectural model?

4. Radius editing on a connected rotunda (model B)

Question: when the radius changes, do the neighbours follow, or does the edit refuse?

5. Face this wall, and Unroll the room

Question: is elevation editing a new mode, or two readings the shell already names?

Decided: no “Focus” mode and no new surface. Face is an explicit Look action that Unroll never triggers. Unroll the room starts at the selected Wall and reads clockwise from inside; it depends on neither Face nor connected curves.

6. Walls and overhead: meets, gap, intended

Question: can the first overhead slice be useful with no roof forms and no Wall change?

Recommended. V1 first: it is finished-looking over today's Walls, and it forces the D2 and D3 decisions while the geometry is trivial. A sloped Wall top under today's flat derived lid would look broken.

7. Making a Space: from a Room, from partial Walls, from a floor

Proposal: three entries into one concept. In each, something physical anchors the place.

Decided: a Space exists while a Wall stands on its boundary or a floor lies under it. An opened Room is kept, not retired.

8. Working with a Space: boundary, selection, overlap

Proposal: virtual edges are the only new mark, and they behave like the handles creators already know.

Decided: no overlap on the same surface; containment only as an open space inside a Room; Zones stay reserved for broader scopes.

9. Interior Reveal: Lift and the near-wall cutaway

Proposal: one reading the creator enters (Lift) and one automatic behaviour the creator can switch (cutaway).

Decided: Lift ships before the first authored ceiling. The cutaway gets a switch and a hold key, and nothing more.

10. Slice: deliberate horizontal and vertical cuts

Proposal: build the Slice PLATE already specifies, Level cut first, and rank it ahead of the automatic cutaway.

Decided: deterministic before heuristic. In planning scope for this batch; not in its immediate implementation.

11. The rotunda: whole and components

Proposal: the whole is the Room and carries Radius; each stretch of wall is a real Wall; nothing is fabricated in either direction.

Decided: the Room is the rotunda. Edits that would break the circle refuse; Release round is the explicit workaround.
