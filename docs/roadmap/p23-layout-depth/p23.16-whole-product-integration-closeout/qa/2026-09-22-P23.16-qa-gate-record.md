## 5. Findings and their dispositions

### 5.1 Unlit-scene preview blackness — NOT a defect (owner ruling 2026-09-22)

The visitor preview surface rendered black for a Scene with no light entity. **Owner ruling:**
this is a lighting expectation, not a rendering defect — with no lighting authored, there is
nothing to see. Recorded as current product behaviour; no correction proposed, and no gate
claim depends on it. (The preview surface itself is reachable and renders its overlay + canvas,
recorded under M1.)

### 5.2 M14 row 2 — the field does NOT cancel on an external history transaction

Live evidence in M14 matches the P23.13 record verbatim: the pending text survives an external
undo while the commit stays honest. This was an explicit P23.13 owner decision ("record it,
don't take the fix"). It is therefore **not a new defect and not a gate blocker**; the
verification plan's M14 wording ("a field cancels on an external history transaction") named a
behaviour that was never landed, and it has been corrected to describe the verified behaviour.
The underlying display-only staleness remains recorded post-P23 debt, owned by whoever next
owns Plan numeric entry.

not owed for this closure and the owner accepts they may drift. E5's legacy claim rests on the
automated compatibility evidence instead. This reduces the number of owed owner rows rather
