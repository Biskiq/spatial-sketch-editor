Satisfying these exit criteria makes P23 **CLOSABLE, not closed.** Closing P23 is a
separate owner-invoked `phase-closeout` procedure that owns P-level status, the cycle
transition and the close preflight (cycle is `WAITING`, so its target is computed, never
assumed).

## 0. Method

- **Reuse accepted evidence.** A slice's accepted state is its closeout record +
  per-seam suites, not a re-derivation. P23.14 and P23.15 keep it in their QA stubs
  (full bodies behind the recorded `git show` anchors); earlier slices keep it in their
  landed plans' Status/acceptance sections and their suites.
- **Two evidence classes, never conflated.** Automated lane/command output and live
  interactive rows are recorded as separate columns. A row that was not exercised is
  `owed` — never inferred from source inspection.
- **Re-run only on invalidating change.** Automated lanes are re-run at the gate because
  the gate is the last whole-product statement, not to re-litigate each slice. No
  per-slice acceptance is repeated otherwise.
- **Falsification rule.** A check that falsifies landed behaviour is an implementation
  change: it reopens review and **stops** the gate. A check that falsifies only a
  closeout *document* is repaired under exit criterion E8.

## 1. The representative fixture (F1)

One composed canonical project, used by both the automated and the live lane so
identities can be compared across surfaces:

- **Layout:** canonical wall-first `LayoutDocument` — one drawn Room, Walls at degree
  2/3/4, a curved leg, a Wall-hosted Opening, one `partition` Wardrobe run, authored
  names/identities. It is assembled from the same canonical writers the product uses.
- **Scene:** pre-seeded canonical world-local `SceneDocument` (`formatVersion: 1`, no
  `roomId` anywhere) imported through the Document menu.

The pre-seeded Scene is **required**, not a shortcut: TD-1 (documented below) makes a
fresh canonical project unable to author its first Camera node. The fixture path is the
one P23.14's QA harness already used; P23.16 extends it forward through the loop rather
than inventing a second harness.
