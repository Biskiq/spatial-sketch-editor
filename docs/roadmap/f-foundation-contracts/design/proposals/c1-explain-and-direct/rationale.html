1 The scenario, runnable

A pump bay with two instances of one pump definition. The rotor runs on its own clock; the casing supports opening (one representation channel, casing.separation). Two Experiences — How it works and Service check — share the world. Everything in the brief’s six steps is interactive; the Presenter (J) replays each step as an instant, resettable fixture so a reviewer can jump anywhere and keep going by hand.

Alternate paths are live at every step: cancel capture, choose Yield or Stop instead of Handoff, Undo/Redo any accepted change, keep a locked frame as authored (acknowledge), switch a cut to travel and back, exit preview mid-run. The Presenter’s Reset fixture restores revision 14; its Simulate buttons inject another writer or a failing capture at any moment.

3 Lifetimes, terminology and scope rules

P26’s “view only” distinction is kept and extended to four lifetimes. Each has one visual species, used identically in tags, chips, the Paper chip, rows and the run panel.

Source
Accepted project truth. Solid ink. Changes only through acceptance; the status bar shows its revision.
View only
Session. Dashed slate. Inspection, free look, cutaway, scrub position, selection. Gone on return; never captured implicitly.
Stop 2
Authored presentation. Plum tab naming the occurrence that owns it (a Stop’s held state, a use). Not a Scene default.
Run #3
Active run. Hatched. Visitor handoffs, stopped uses, held poses in preview. Discarded on exit; Restart is a new number.

Terms, as the creator sees them

Experience
A visitor-facing explanation over the shared world (“How it works”, “Service check”). Owns order and continuation.
Stop
An occurrence in an Experience, with its own id (OCC-n), words and beats. A Stop is about a subject; two Stops about pump A are two visits (“visit 1 of 2”), never one object shown twice.
Still moment
A Stop with a view, held states and words and no timing. Most explanations never need more.
Beat
One line of a timed Stop: shot (a Camera view, by cut or travel), use (a performance on a subject), say (words), hold. Placed by relation: start · then · with · at cue.
Performance
A reusable typed resource (“Open casing”, 2.4 s, 0.60 m). Lives in Reusable, not in any Stop.
Use
One invocation of a performance in a Stop, on one subject. May override speed or parameters for this use only.
View / route
Camera-owned. A view is locked or follows its subject (assisted). A cut needs no route; travel must resolve one or show a gap.
World activity
Ambient Scene behavior (rotor spin). Shown in every lens; no Stop, run or visitor can pause, cut or restart it.

5 From states to timing — two ways compared

The brief asks for at least two progressions. Both were built against the same Stop and remain in the prototype as lenses, so they can be compared on identical content.

A · Beats — ordered relations Committed default

startShot A detail
withSay …
at cueCut to B side
thenUse Open casing ×0.6
  • Grows from the still moment by adding lines; a still Stop is a Beats Stop with one shot and held states.
  • Authored meaning is relation, not seconds: changing a performance duration or words length re-derives times without edits.
  • Repeated visits, object-only uses and world activity read as sentences, not track positions.
  • Weakness: exact overlaps and “how long is this?” are hard to see. Hypothesis

B · Clock — derived ruler

A detailB side (cut)
Open casing ×0.6
Say …
Rotor spin · own clock ↤ ↦
  • Shows duration, overlap and the cut instant; scrubbing seeks the Stop through the same evaluator as preview.
  • Dragging a use’s end changes that use’s speed — never an absolute time — so the Beats relations stay true.
  • World activity overhangs both edges: it is not the Stop’s to trim.
  • Rejected as the authority: absolute times would break on every content change and pull every task onto a professional timeline.

Decision. Beats is the authored model and the default lens once a Stop is timed; Clock is a read-mostly lens over the same beats, offering seek and local retime only. Still stays the default for untimed Stops. This keeps the “one linear timeline” risk named in the brief out of the model while still answering “when?”.

Reduced motion preserves meaning

Follows prefers-reduced-motion or ?motion=reduced, and the Motion toggle in the status bar.

  • Travel becomes a cut with a label naming where the camera went; routes still resolve, so a route gap is still reported.
  • Performances step to their end value with a pulse and label (“Pump A casing opened”); the performance’s Inspector shows its authored reduced-motion alternative.
  • The rotor is shown still and labelled “running”: its independence stays legible.
  • Rejoin is a cut; the summary says so.

Recovery from failure

  • Stale or invalid compound capture: refused whole; no view and no Stop; sheet keeps choices; retry re-plans on the current revision (6.6).
  • Route gap: shown in the beat, map and Review; switching back to a cut repairs it.
  • Locked shot that no longer contains its subject: Review compares locked vs follow frames; “Follow” or “Keep as authored”.
  • Collision after a definition change: repair this use only.
  • Words that no longer match the picture: “needs review” even though every reference resolves.

Semantic assumptions

  • A Stop references Camera views; creating both in one capture is one accepted F.4 action across Camera and Experience.
  • Held states in a Stop are owned contributions of that occurrence, evaluated over the Scene baseline, never written to it.
  • A use carries only invocation data (subject, relation, speed, parameter overrides); the performance definition stays a typed resource.
  • Beat relations (start/then/with/at cue) are Experience-owned; their time mapping evaluates through Camera for shots.
  • Words belong to the Stop, not to a shot; crossing a cut is an explicit author decision (continue or end at the cut).
  • World ambient activity is Scene-owned and runs on its own clock in authoring, preview and visitor.
  • The visitor’s inspection is a Camera profile plus run-scoped channel requests; conflicts use F.3 exclusive resolution.
  • Reusing a Stop in another Experience creates a new occurrence, not a shared one.

11 Hypotheses to test

No testing was conducted. These are what a first session should check:

  • Hypothesis Creators predict capture scope correctly from the sheet before accepting.
  • Hypothesis The scope switch makes “this use only” retiming predictable without knowing override layers.
  • Hypothesis Visit counts and occurrence ids let people tell the two pump A visits apart.
  • Hypothesis People can say what continued (rotor) and what waited (tour, words) after looking around.
  • Hypothesis Beats is sufficient for most explanations; Clock is used mainly to check overlap.
  • Hypothesis Visitors understand the conflict choices without the words handoff/yield.
