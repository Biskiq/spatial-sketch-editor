0 · Status and authority

normative design

This sheet is the design authority for T2 lighting presentation, the Light Cards, Time and Sky, and the shell placement of these controls. Where it disagrees with the documents listed below, this sheet wins. fix.md keeps its diagnosis and execution order and carries a pointer here. The other contracts are not edited by this sheet. Each change they need is listed in §7 and lands with the slice that implements it.

Decided in this revision

  1. Light guides are ray silhouettes. The selected Spot shows its source, aim axis, two to four silhouette rays to the target and a planar cap. No gold footprints are wrapped onto surfaces. The dense variant appears only while a shape is being edited, or when Show influence is on.
  2. Brightness is one logarithmic slider from ⅛× to 8×. Zero and out-of-range stored values are preserved until the user actually writes.
  3. Beam means full cone width. The Card's Beam row shows 2·angle, and Advanced shows the stored half-angle. Beam and Soft edge are separate rows (chosen by the true-size QA in §3). The Spot Card therefore has five everyday rows.
  4. Time sits at the right end of the World-controls bar and opens a compact Sky popover beneath it (chosen by the shell comparison in §4.1).
  5. The floor sundial is the primary Stage control. A rim mark is its fallback when the dial is off-screen or hidden (§4.2).
  6. The Sun's position is a pure function of Time, heading and arc height, so every Time maps to exactly one position. Stage gestures stay on the current arc; only the Time dial wraps (§4.3).
  7. Directional light is retired. The Sky owns the only parallel light. Refusing legacy projects and the format bump are a separate future slice (§7.3).

How to read

  • Each section starts with live prototypes and ends with spec tables: dimensions, states, gestures and keys, fields, boundaries and contract changes.
  • Tags: now can ship with fields that already exist · later T2 needs a T2 slice · WebGPU waits for the renderer refactor · record history, kept only to explain a decision.
  • Ink on the Stage: solid = shows an authored quantity · dashed = derived or reference · faded = hidden behind geometry · gold = the held grip or the selected subject · cobalt = proposal or ghost · red = refusal.
  • Selected UI controls are deep forest and hover is moss, as shipped. Cards stay white.

1 · Identity and marks

kept, refined

Default guide vs dense variant

The dense variant adds rays, the soft-edge ring at angle × (1 − penumbra) and the cap's far arc; a Point adds the floor reach ring. It explains influence, but it is never the default: at rest it would compete with the room and the art.

Stress cases

Selected guide over a future volumetric shaft

Room
Shaft

Result

  • Without the casing, the guide disappears twice. In a dusk or night room, dark ink falls below 2:1 contrast against the walls. Inside a strong shaft, the rays sit on a moving bright texture. Turn the casing off to see both.
  • With the casing (a light stroke 2.4 px wider than the ink, at 70%, under every guide line), the rays and axis read in all three rooms and over both shaft strengths. It adds no colour and no glow.
  • The shaft and the guide disagree, and that is correct. The shaft fades with distance and dust density, so its visible length is shorter than the target distance. The guide always ends at the target distance. Neither is derived from the other.
  • The shaft field (density and dust) lives on the light or the World, not on the guide. It is listed as unresolved in §8, because it waits for WebGPU behind S0.

3 · Light Cards

fields exist

These are the same white Cards, drawn at their true 288 px width (252 px at ≤860 px). Continuous controls use a stacked row: the label and value on one line, then a full-width control below. At 288 px, an inline 74px | slider | value row leaves about 134 px for the slider, and labelled stops collide. A stacked row gives 264 px.

Card width
Beam control
Stored brightness
Loads a project value. It is not a write.

True-size QA (measured live in this page)

(a) One dual-thumb row. The outer thumb is the full width and the inner thumb the full-brightness core. It is compact, but the thumbs overlap whenever the soft edge is small, and the soft edge reads as degrees although it is stored as a fraction.
(b) Beam and Soft edge rows chosen. Each row is one stored field with its own unit, every thumb is always reachable, and the ticks have room. The cost is one more row (Spot: five everyday rows). This is recorded as the amendment to fix.md §6 growth rule 4.

Advanced (Spot)

  • Only one group is open at a time. Each closed group shows its summary on the right.
  • Typed fields commit on Enter and restore on Esc. Blur writes nothing. A value outside the range is refused at the field with the reason.
  • Shape shows the stored half-angle with its full-width echo, so the Card's Beam and the stored field can never be confused.
  • Brightness holds the exact intensity in candela, including 0 and values outside the slider.
  • There is no Direction group: Directional is retired.

4 · Time, Sky and the sundial

S1 · S2

Sky is World mood, not a tool. Time is the one Sky value people change often, so it is always visible as a control. Opening it gives a compact Sky popover that never covers the open Card or most of the Stage.

A · World-controls bar, right end chosen. Time joins the controls that change how the World looks (view, grid, measure). It is set off by a divider and gap, so it reads as World state, not a viewing toggle. Sky drops beneath it, toward the Stage's quiet top band.
B · Bottom dock, right end considered. Time sits with the authoring tools, but Sky is not a tool and has no armed state. The popover rises into the middle of the Stage, where the work is. It competes with the Library tier, which opens in the same place.

4.2 · The Stage sundial: floor dial vs rim mark

Approach

Drag the shadow tip, the sunlight on the floor, or the rim mark: the Sun moves along today's arc and stops at sunrise and sunset. Drag the dial's compass ring to turn the arc (heading). Only the Time dial in the Sky popover crosses into twilight and night.

Morning and afternoon are on opposite sides of the noon line, so every Time has exactly one position, and every reachable position has exactly one Time. Solar noon is 13:00 (summer time). The function is pure: no date, latitude or clock is involved, and the same project always renders the same Sun.

5 · Artwork and placement

kept from #314
Width
  • The selection is a 1 px gold outline 7 px outside the frame, so thin frames stay visible.
  • Size tags stay on while the Artwork is selected; a corner drag adds the framed size.
  • Card order is Size, Hang, Frame, then actions. Position and rotation live under Advanced.

Moving along walls: ends, openings and corners

Pointer
  • The Artwork follows the pointer while its whole frame backs onto one wall.
  • Where it would overhang a wall end, an opening or a corner, it stops at the last place it fitted: Stops at the wall edge.
  • Over a gap, a doorway or the floor, it waits where it was. Once it fits on the next wall, it jumps there and takes that wall's direction.
  • Shift at press moves it freely in its own plane. Flat Artwork tangent to a curved Wall stays valid.
  • Release commits the final candidate from the pointer-up event, even when a preview frame was still queued.

8 · Recommendations, open decisions, handoff

Recommendations

  1. Ship the ray guide, the emitter pick target and the stacked Card rows together. They use only existing fields, and they remove today's two worst readings: the invisible light and the clipped Card.
  2. Put Time at the bar's right end now, even while it is inert, so S2 adds dials without moving anything.
  3. Run S0's tone-mapping decision before tuning Brightness stops. The stops are a UI mapping, so stored values survive.
  4. Build the floor sundial with the rim fallback in S2, and keep both out of selection and the Card.
  5. Pick up the Directional retirement as its own small Tier 2 PR.

Unresolved owner decisions

  1. The final preset set, and how it maps to today's daylight, golden-hour, dusk and the neutral preset.
  2. Whether the Sun always casts shadows (PLATE L980).
  3. Moon strength and colour under More.
  4. Default Shadow state for a new Spot.
  5. The volumetric field: on the light, on the World, or both (S0-gated).
  6. Group Card scope for light rigs (G1).
  7. Where Show influence lives: ⚙ viewing options or a Card foot action.
