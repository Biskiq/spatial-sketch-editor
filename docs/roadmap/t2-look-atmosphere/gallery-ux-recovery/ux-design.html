Light Cards

Left: today, with the problems outlined in red. Right: proposed. Choose a kind.

Advanced

The Card's More becomes Advanced. It opens inside the same Card as a short list of groups. One group is open at a time; each closed group shows its current value, so nothing has to be opened just to read it. New features later join a group as one more row, or arrive as a new group at the end. The everyday Card never grows.

Moving along walls: ends, openings and corners (A4)

Pointer

Plan view. Drag the pointer along the wall, through the doorway and round the corner.

  • The Artwork follows the pointer while its whole frame backs onto one wall.
  • Where it would overhang a wall end, an opening or a corner, it stops at the last place it fitted: Stops at the wall edge.
  • Over a gap, a doorway or the floor it waits where it was.
  • Once it fits on the next wall it jumps there and takes that wall's direction. It never floats across the corner.
  • Shift at press: move freely in its own plane, as today. Free-standing Artwork attaches to the first wall it fits.
  • Flat Artwork tangent to a curved Wall remains valid; it does not need to bend to pass the wall-fit test.
  • Release commits the final candidate from the pointer-up event, even when a preview frame was still queued.

Icon sets

The same eight jobs at 22 px. Judge them on the Card, the dial plate and the dark dock. The set must not depend on colour alone: every glyph keeps a distinct silhouette at 16 px.

A · Instrument recommended

24 grid · 1.5 round

Open, measured forms that extend the existing shell vocabulary. Best match for the Card, the mono values and the gold selection ink.

B · Gallery marks

24 grid · 1.8 square

More graphic, squared terminals. Reads as signage at a glance, but sits less comfortably beside the 1.5 px shell glyphs.

C · Duotone

24 grid · 1.4 + 14% fill

Quiet fill behind the stroke. Helps the Advanced group rows scan faster, but a filled set needs a second token for hover and disabled states.

Directional · several lights on one dial

Option A recommended

Selected name sits at the dial centre; other lights are 6 px clickable ticks on their From angles. Hover names a tick; click selects that light.

Clean after one host addition: a click-only mark role. It does not move either bead.

Option B

Other lights become numbered badges; a compact roster below the dial maps each number to a name. Selects by badge or roster.

More markup and a second list; also needs click-only marks. Best when there are many Directional lights.

No ticks at all: the selected name stays on the dial and every other Directional light lives in a two-column roster below it.

Simplest to draw and the easiest to make keyboard-reachable; less direct than pointing at a direction.

Artwork refusal and the Advanced Aim readout

Artwork · refusal recommended

A. When the frame does not fit, draw the same ghost in refusal red dashed and show the planner's own reason. Never invent a reason; if the host has no fit refusal, the ghost keeps proposal ink and the Card says why.

Requires one fit predicate shared by hover and release. Cleanly extractable once the predicate exists.

Artwork · hold

B. Keep the last fitted pose, tint only its outline, and put "Stops at the wall edge" beside the pointer. No red ghost.

Closest to the current hold behaviour, but does not distinguish a refusal from a pending candidate.

Artwork · chip only

C. No ghost change; a status chip states the reason until the pointer moves.

Cheapest, but the pointer and the refused pose are disconnected.

Aim · surface intercept recommended

A. Advanced Aim reads the point where the direction meets the picked surface, with the same three fields and an Aim · 2.64 m label. Editing the fields still writes direction only.

Needs the pick result already carried by the Spot mark. This removes the 3 m fallback without adding a stored target.

Already shipped and truthful; still reads as a point in space.
