One Look, three reaches

The Sky sets the mood. Finishes say what things are made of. Lights say where to look. Each has one home in the shell and a curated start.

World reach

Sky

Preset, then Time, Direction, Haze, Brightness. A Look changes any World's atmosphere and treatment in one click without rewriting a Finish.

Surface reach

Finish

Pick a tile, adjust colour and Sheen, open More for your own image, scale and exact values. Shared by default, unique by one explicit choice.

Attention reach

Light

Point, spot and directional emitters placed and edited on the Stage. Light it does the first one for you.

And the work on show

The three reaches serve a fourth thing that is not appearance: Artwork, a creator's own paintings, photographs and graphics as first-class objects. It is in this batch because it is what the Sky, Finishes and Lights are for, and because "hang it, frame it, light it, walk up to it" can be judged by eye.

Curated surface, open engine

Every everyday control is a named view onto a stored physical value: Satin is roughness 0.6, Warm is a colour, Golden hour is a sun angle and a sky. The model never stores the label and never closes the list. Depth is revealed in three levels that are the same in every home: Pick a tile, Adjust up to four named controls, open More for exact values. Same writer, same undo, no new panel.

Where the image enters decides what it is

Dropped on the Stage or imported in Library › Artwork: an Artwork object. Chosen in a Finish row: a map on a Finish. Both read the same stored image; neither shares identity with the other.

Limits in this batch

On the wall is a placement snap, not a bond: the artwork does not follow a wall that is later moved, like every object today. No picture glass. No editing yet. Artwork is lit and matte, and S0 must prove its colours stay true.

Seams for an Artwork Studio

A source union (image now, drawing later), a reserved Edit action on the Card, a New drawing entry in the Library, and the same scoped-entry pattern as Object Studio with a flat reading of the picture plane. Trim (crop, straighten) should come before any brush.

Groups, one rule set

  1. A group is flat membership: members keep world positions; visitors see them as if ungrouped.
  2. Click selects the group; a second click selects the member.
  3. Move, rotate, Duplicate and Erase act on the group rigidly, so a spot keeps its aim on its painting.
  4. Light it groups the new light with its subject. Duplicate along a wall gives a lit row; Ungroup when one light serves several works.

Fixtures and rigs

Emitters ship in this batch. A fixture is an object grouped with an emitter; models are an asset decision and follow later. A saved rig is a saved group, inserted as a detached copy. A live-linked rig should arrive together with the same capability for objects.

Look through is realised as a Camera inspection pose through the one Camera authority and writes only the light's transform, once, on Done. These three decisions are stated as I understood them; I found no written record to check against.

Rendering truth: S0 is a gate

Sky-derived reflections are a hypothesis until measured. The interface must not promise what the renderer cannot show, so each promise leaves S0 as go, go with a renamed label, or wait.

Known limits, stated in the product

  1. Sky reflections are not mirrors. Surfaces reflect sky and ground, never each other or the room. The top Sheen stop is High gloss, not Mirror.
  2. Indoors is the hard case. A glossy floor in an enclosed room would reflect open sky. S0 shows it and chooses: accept, damp by enclosure, or schedule an interior capture.
  3. Opacity is not glass. No refraction, no tinted shadow. See-through bases ship as Clear · Frosted · Tinted; the word Glass only if the gate images earn it.
  4. Glow lights nothing and without bloom reads as bright colour. The Card says so.

S0 exit criteria

  1. Pipeline audit: colour space, tone mapping, exposure, light units as one agreed set. This alone unblocks Lights.
  2. PBR chart: roughness × metal grid and textured sets, each preset, outdoors and in an enclosed room.
  3. Live Sky: sun, sky and haze follow a drag every frame; reflection rebuild measured and its policy chosen.
  4. Performance: frame time and load on a laptop iGPU and a phone-class device, Stage and visit. Sets the shadow budget.
  5. Paper / Living: Paper stays neutral; D4 decided from images.
  6. Parity: Stage, snapshot, Preview and visit resolve the same values; no editor code in visitor chunks.
  7. Artwork colour fidelity: a reference image under neutral light beside its source file, with no difference an artist would see.

Not ruled out, each behind its own measurement: interior reflection capture · area lights · transmission · a post pass · baked or cached shadows. The contract that keeps them open: one resolved-look module with a quality input, one material factory, no slice bypassing either.

A realistic batch

Capability slices, not implementation plans. Two things start now; Lights and Artwork form the first milestone. A plan is written only where the workflow asks for one.

P0, bounded

The spatial route is 5,901 lines with 97 imports and every look slice would edit it. P0 leaves one construction of a look module and four mount points (Card stratum, Stage mark layer, Library shelf, Sky popover), and proves the seam by moving the Sky and material-pick wiring that exist today. It is not a route refactor.

Handoff to implementation planning

Plan first: S0 (invention risk), A1 (format bump and release pinning), S1 and S5a (Tier 2 format), S8 (licence). Everything else is a Tier 0–1 PR described in its own description. Open schema questions for the S1 plan are listed in the README; none is decided here.
