One Look, three reaches

The Sky sets the mood. Finishes say what things are made of. Lights say where to look. Each has one home in the shell, a curated start and at most four everyday controls.

World reach

Sky

Preset, then Time, Direction, Haze, Brightness. The sky itself becomes the reflection and ambient light, so mood and surfaces can never disagree.

Surface reach

Finish

Base + colour + Sheen. Metal is a kind of base, not a slider. Shared by default, unique by one explicit choice.

Attention reach

Light

Fixtures aimed at a target you drag on a surface. Warmth, Brightness and Beam in named stops. Light it does the first one for you.

What changes the order of work

Today nothing in the renderer reflects anything: a brass with metalness 0.85 has no environment to mirror. Polished, Metal and Glass cannot look right whatever controls we add, so sky-derived reflections come first and need no UI at all.

Four journeys

Counts are interactions after the geometry exists.

5

Gallery lighting

  1. Sky → Dusk
  2. Select the painting → Card Light it: a Picture spot lands on the ceiling at 30°, 3000 K, shadow on
  3. Drag the aim target to the canvas centre
  4. Beam → Narrow
  5. Duplicate along the wall for the row
4

Product showcase

  1. Sky → Paper (studio-neutral reflections)
  2. Select product → Finish swatch → Library › Polished brass
  3. Light it ▾ Showcase: key, fill and rim in one undo
  4. Sheen → Satin to calm the highlight
5

Architectural daylight

  1. Sky → Daylight
  2. Drag the sun mark round the plan rim until light rakes through the window
  3. Time → 16:00
  4. Paint Oak, Room scope, on the floor; Plaster on walls
  5. Shadows → Soft
4

Deliberately stylized

  1. Library › Looks → Riso: flat two-tone sky, crisp tinted shadows, five colours
  2. Paint palette colours, Matte, onto forms
  3. Change the Accent slot: every follower moves
  4. Sky › Save as look

Implementation batch

Two foundations, then three slices that can run in parallel. Lights come first for value: they need no schema change.

Deferred

Area lights · HDRIs · glass transmission · bloom or any post pass · outlines / toon · per-face finishes on forms (T5) · decals · texture upload as a Finish image · look A/B snapshots · per-Stop lighting in Experience (the highest-value follow-up, but an Experience design).
