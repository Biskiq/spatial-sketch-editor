4Shape a path directly

Now: points can only be added by arming a dock tool, then clicking the route — and View markers win the click, so short routes can't be edited at all. "0 points" and a disabled Straighten button are all the Card offers.

Editing

  • Selecting a Travel puts its route above everything: other Views dim and stop taking clicks until you click away.
  • White discs are points: drag to move, double-click or Delete to remove. The small + between points: drag it out to make a new point. The two ends are locked to their Views (dashed ring).
  • No mode, no dock tool, no "Done". Every drag is one Undo. Straighten (Card) removes all points.
