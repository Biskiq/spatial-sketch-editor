## 1. What the gate decided, and how

```text
M-3 (identity)  A predecessor Room whose own boundary cycle still exists as a candidate face IS
                that face's owner, by authored identity alone; and a face owned by one Room
                matches NO other Room. The oracle required, on every gate row: every claim IS a
                union the pair proof makes; a claimed Room unions with ITS OWN face and nothing
                else; a claimed face belongs to exactly its one owner; every derived refusal
                (a claimed Room against a different Room's claimed face) is NOT a union; and an
                ambiguous face (two Rooms naming it — the merge shape) is never settled.
M-2 (extent)    A pair the structural scope leaves out must have ALL FOUR inputs unchanged from
                the baseline: predecessor polygon, witness, face polygon (matched by canonical
                key), both D-12 component labels. The extent is derived from changed/removed
                Junction and Wall records, Walls incident to changed Junctions, Rooms whose
                boundary refs touch them, and faces that are new-key or whose polygon moved.
MUTATION        Two whole-document mutations prove the exclusion conservative: moving an
                excluded face's Junction by +0.5 m moves that face INTO the extent (and its
                polygon provably differs), and renaming an operated Room's whole boundary (the
                direct D-12 row) moves that Room INTO the extent with its label changed.
OUTCOME         NO inequality on any row, both mutation proofs pass. The conditions are sound
                where real plans live.
```
