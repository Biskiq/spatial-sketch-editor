## 1. Problem, and the evidence that motivates it

```text
1.1  ONE RULE, FOUR SCOPES, AND THEY DISAGREE (F-C7, F-C1, F-C6). The same crossing rule is applied with
     four different subjects, so an operation can ADMIT what a later gate REJECTS:
       Room duplicate      isolation → codec → validateBatchWallTopology (chord-exact)   curve crossing NOT checked
       Room move           codec → validateWallFirstTopology (full) → portals            checked, REJECTS
       Wall chain authoring auto-adopt/auto-node → validateChainTopology (full)          checked, rejects the curved case
       import              codec only                                                    NOT checked
       compile             per-Room boundary geometry only                               BLIND (F-C2)
     The consequence is the reported defect: a duplicate or an import admits a curved inter-Room crossing,
     and the NEXT unrelated edit is then rejected and blamed for pre-existing geometry.

1.2  THE OPERATOR'S DIRECTION CHANGES THE SUBJECT, NOT THE RULE (D-1, §2.10). Under Option E an
     intersection between entities that are NOT in the same connected component is intentionally
     permitted geometry. The rule stays one rule; its subject becomes component-scoped.

1.3  CONNECTIVITY IS ALREADY DERIVED, AND JUNCTION IDENTITY IS ITS AUTHORITY (§2.11 Q1/Q3). A group is
     the transitive closure over shared boundary Junction ids. Portal relations are DEPENDENT — validated
     against derived adjacency, never constitutive of it.

1.4  THE COINCIDENCE RULE IS THE HARD PART, AND IT IS NOT A SCHEMA GAP (D-9, §2.11.2). Today
     `duplicate_junction_point` rejects ANY two Junctions within 1e-9 ANYWHERE in the document, and every
     authoring path that resolves a coincident endpoint ADOPTS the existing Junction. Both guards act
     document-wide. Two of the owner's requirements — duplicate a Room at exactly the same coordinates,
     and independent Walls with identical endpoint coordinates — land directly on them.

1.5  THE AUDIT WAS INCOMPLETE IN TWO WAYS (§2.11 Q7, §2.11.3), and both are THIS slice's obligations:
       GAP 1  `connectedRoomIds` is ROOM-ONLY. A standalone Wall, a partition stub or an unenclosed chain
              contributes nothing to it, so the thing Option E must classify cannot be classified by the
              helper the plan originally assumed.
       GAP 2  face-extraction correspondence is GEOMETRIC. `buildCorrespondenceComponents` unions a
              predecessor Room with a candidate face on interior containment OR shared interior area, and
              `polygonsShareInteriorArea`'s adjacency guard is tolerance-based with an explicit
              identical/nested-ring fallback. Coincident or contained graph-INDEPENDENT Rooms therefore
              already collapse into ONE correspondence component.

1.6  UN-8 is closed by this slice's existence: whether the chord-only duplicate gate was a deliberate
     P23.4 scope choice or an omission is answered by re-scoping it, and the misleading comment at
     `layout-duplicate.ts:196-200` is corrected HERE (D-8), alongside the behaviour it describes.
```

## 4. Proposed mechanism, and the alternatives

        Room move while the general test serves the gates. ALTERNATIVE: generalise `connectedRoomIds`

        scope, and changing the helper to serve two consumers invites a regression in the one that works.

        applying the UNCHANGED predicate. One predicate, one verdict vocabulary, four consistent scopes.
M-3a-3  COMPONENT-SCOPED COINCIDENCE. `duplicate_junction_point` is evaluated WITHIN a component.
        WHERE that scoping lives — a scoped form of the existing predicate, or a distinct gate — is an
        implementation choice, not an owner decision (D-9).
M-3a-4  AUTHORING INTENT SPLIT. Adoption/crossing-split is retained for an operation EXTENDING a group
        and withdrawn for INDEPENDENT placement. The discriminator is the operation's declared intent,
        not the geometry. ALTERNATIVE: remove adoption entirely. REJECTED — it breaks T13 and the
        within-group authoring that E's containment rule exists to protect.
M-3a-5  JOIN/CONNECT CONTRACT (PLANNING LEVEL ONLY, D-10). Named interface and invariants: an explicit,
        attributable operation that may adopt/merge Junctions between components; atomic; a history
        entry; no silent geometry change. NO UI, mode or command is designed or implemented here. F8's
        POSITIVE HALF IS THIS CONTRACT (AM-3) and therefore carries NO test in this slice — only F8's
        negative half (no IMPLICIT join from any path) is asserted (A-2).
