## 0. Why the report is not edited

The report is historical research evidence. Rewriting its tags in place would break the rule this phase
holds everywhere else — corrections go in an addendum, never by silently rewriting a record — and would
also destroy the ability to check the archived file against what was actually produced. So the report
keeps its tags verbatim and this companion carries the readable form.

**Tags that appear, and what they mean:**

| Tag form | Occurrences | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `<Link url="…" title="…"/>` | 12 | A project or documentation source, as a client-specific inline link. The URL and title are real; the tag is not Markdown. |
| `<Cite ref="turnNsearchM"/>` | 11 | A session-local search reference from the authoring session. **It does not resolve on the web.** See §2. |
| `<Cite refs={["turnNsearchM", …]}/>` | 6 | The same, grouping two ids. |

## 4. Evidence-label vocabulary used by the report

These labels travel with every citation into the synthesis and are needed to read §B and §F correctly:

```text
SV       source-verified at a pinned revision — the strongest label the report issues
DOC      documented but not independently verified in source
INF      applicability inference — the report's own reading, not established by the source
UNKNOWN  mechanism not established
```

The synthesis's rule for consuming these labels is **U-1**: a mechanism is never admitted on
`external precedent` alone. Admission requires an equivalence proof answerable from Museum Editor's own
code, and AB-8 returns a mechanism to `unresolved` if its only justification is a precedent that is
`INF`, or that could not be established at all.
