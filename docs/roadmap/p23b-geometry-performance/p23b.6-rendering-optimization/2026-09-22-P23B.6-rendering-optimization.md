## Delivered

P23B.6 shipped on the dedicated `P23B.6` branch (one PR: #93) after the owner reviewed, corrected
and then ACCEPTED it (2026-09-26) with no remaining blocker. Two mechanisms landed, both
conditional on S1 and both admitted mechanically:

- **S-R — compiled-generation reactive representation (RETAIN).** The compiled `geometry` and the
  projected `model` are wholesale-replaced `$state.raw` references, so a generation is a value
  replaced atomically instead of a deep proxy tree rebuilt on first read. The single writer
  (`installWallGeometry`) and both mounts (`EditorApp`, `MuseumEditorApp`) keep their contracts;
  the S6 proxy mapping stays dormant in place. A-2 met: the same-protocol Plan `plan-render-model`
  term moved 2.7/3.3 → 0.1/0.2 ms straight, 4.9/6.3 → 0.2/0.3 all-curved, 3.2/3.7 → 0.1/0.2
  owner-curved (p50/p95, advisory).
- **S3 M-3m — cross-generation prepared Wall-mesh reuse (ADAPT; owner-authorized in PR #93 on
  2026-09-26).** One renderer-free, Svelte-state-free preparation module owns the moved
  `buildWallMeshesByRoom` path and the EXISTING weakly keyed `derivedWallMeshes` generation cache —
  no second cache, no new retention. On a miss the caller supplies the currently installed
  generation as the only reference; a Wall is reused iff its whole compiled Wall record, floor
  elevation, resolved ends and builder-signature tag are value-equal, its reference build succeeded
  and produced no issues (issues are recomputed per generation). Legacy Room meshes never cross
  generations; no compiled-object identity and no view state ever admits reuse. The structural
  plain-data verdict is computed once per generation and stored with its prepared set (tests + one
  DEV-only assertion); production trusts `layout-core`'s compiler-owned plain-data output, so the
  per-Wall comparison performs no reflective walk. Guard recheck: net **positive on all four
  accepted single-Wall fixtures** (straight +8.821, target-curved +8.354, all-curved +12.997,
  owner-curved +7.362 ms p50), with OR-HIT reuse of 36/36/37/37 of 40 Walls and all OR-9 rows
  refusing.
- **S1 five-layer attribution** of the post-P23B.7 residual (S1a node probes, S1b one clean browser
  capture) with the mechanical admission table, plus **S2 behaviour pins** (mesh/raw-Plan output,
  the H-1…H-5 harness on a `$state`-backed preview).
- **Retention proof H-1…H-6** and the recorded **H-7 contract for P26** (§6): a canonical
  generation is strongly held only by live state and document history; prepared meshes and future
  derived artifacts are weakly keyed by their generation and never keep it alive; shared meshes
  collect with their last generation; GPU resources stay under renderer-lifecycle ownership and
  never enter history or snapshots.
- **2026-09-26 review corrections:** H-5 now drives the mounted production `LayoutPreviewScene`
  lifecycle (edit, undo/redo, branch, 3D↔Plan switch, remount, unmount) with only Threlte's
  scene-graph sinks stubbed, and its mutation proof (disabling the production Wall cleanup) fails
  it; H-6 lives in the pairwise-disjoint heavy lane with `--expose-gc` and `P23B6_REQUIRE_GC=1`,
  failing loudly instead of skipping when GC is unavailable.

S4 (`BufferGeometry` adapter reuse) and S5 (GPU/presented-frame) were DROPPED under §5.0 — the
`LayoutPreviewScene`/Canvas lifecycle they would optimize is the boundary P26 replaces — and
Plan-side SVG/affine work was dropped for the same reason.
