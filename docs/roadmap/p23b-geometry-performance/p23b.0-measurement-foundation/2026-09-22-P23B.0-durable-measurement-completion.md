## 2. The identity problem this slice must solve honestly

```text
The historical payloads do not exist in the repository. P23B.0-durable CANNOT "commit E2/E3" from the
historical pass — the bytes were never serialized and E3 was repaired in memory. Any claim to the
contrary would be a provenance fabrication. Two honest branches, decided by owner input:

BRANCH-R  (RECOVERY — preferred if available)
  the owner re-supplies the original 40-curved-Wall export; its SHA-256 is compared against the
  reported E2 digest 37a857690ca255e0355f08519a195575d12e53b43ca9bb2f7820b18bb36dc27f.
    · match     → commit it as the NEGATIVE CORRECTNESS fixture `E2` (never a performance baseline),
                  and derive the clean variant by a COMMITTED, deterministic repair whose steps are
                  recorded; the repaired document becomes `E3` with its own content hash and identity.
    · mismatch  → treat as a NEW payload: give it a new id, and do NOT label it E2 or E3.

BRANCH-C  (CONSTRUCTION — the fallback, and the only option if no payload is available)
  construct the fixtures deterministically in code from a committed spec:
    · `E1`  12 curved Walls (3 Rooms x 4 cubic boundary Walls), valid by construction
    · `N-2` a 40-Wall fixture with genuine curved inter-Room crossings (the E2 SHAPE), valid-by-design
             NONE of it — used ONLY to pin rejection/admission behaviour
    · `E3C` a 40-Wall all-curved VALID fixture, valid by construction
  `N-2` and `E3C` are labeled for what they are: a constructed reproduction of the SHAPE, not the
  historical bytes. The historical E1/E2/E3 remain what the P23B.1 plan §2 says they are: reported,
  uncommitted, unrecoverable measurement evidence.
```

**Rule that holds under both branches:** E2 (or `N-2`) is a **negative correctness fixture only**. No
pass may be taken against it. No ratio may be computed from the historical passes. No durable artifact
may be labelled `E3` unless it has a serialized identity — in BRANCH-C it is `E3C`, a different,
honestly-named fixture.

## 3. The control matrix (this is what makes a size/curvature claim possible)

The historical passes varied **both** document size (12 vs 40 Walls) **and** curvature (mostly-straight
probe vs all-curved) **and** environment. That is why no scaling law is admissible (harvest UN-4,
synthesis M-2). P23B.0-durable replaces that with a matrix measured in ONE environment, ONE session,
ONE sampling config:

| Axis | Levels |
|---|---|
| document size | 12 Walls (3 Rooms × 4) · 40 Walls (10 Rooms × 4) |
| curvature | all-straight · target-Wall-only-curved · **all-curved** |

```text
Deliverable: every cell of the matrix is a committed fixture with a stable id, and every cell has a
recorded measurement under the SAME provenance block. Only then may a statement like "curvature, not
size, drives stage X" be made — and only for the measured environment, which must be named.

Also required: a straight-Wall control for the SAME room/wall shape as each curved fixture, so
"curved vs straight" is one variable and "small vs large" is another.
```

## 6. Risks

```text
R-1  PROVENANCE DRIFT. A baseline recorded on a dirty tree is not reproducible from HEAD alone. The
     existing `treeDirty`/`contentHash` fields already handle this; the slice must record a CLEAN-TREE
     baseline for the authoritative one.
R-2  FIXTURE LEGITIMACY. A constructed fixture (BRANCH-C) is not the historical payload. If it is ever
     called "E2" or "E3" the phase has fabricated provenance. Naming discipline is the mitigation.
R-3  METRIC PROLIFERATION. Extending `BenchMetricName` invites unbounded growth. Add only metrics a
     slice will actually consume; every added name is a maintenance surface.
R-4  MEASUREMENT BECOMING A PRODUCT CHANGE. The DEV-gated marks must stay behind the flag; a mark in a
     hot loop that is not compiled out can itself perturb the measurement.
R-5  ENFORCED-BUDGET PERTURBATION. Adding cached key material or new fixtures can move
     `cache-key-code-units`/`compiled-memory` for existing tiers. Any movement must be explained, not
     re-baselined to silence a failure. Re-recording a budget to make a red test green is forbidden.
R-6  SAMPLING HONESTY. Wall-clock samples on a developer machine are advisory. Report p50/p95 with the
     sample count and the warmup; never one run.
```
