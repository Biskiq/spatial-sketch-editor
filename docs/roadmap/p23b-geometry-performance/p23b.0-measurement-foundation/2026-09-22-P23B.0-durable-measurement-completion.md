## 3. The control matrix (this is what makes a size/curvature claim possible)

**Correction to an earlier overstatement in this plan.** The historical passes did NOT vary only size
and curvature together: **both passes included a SAME-SHAPE STRAIGHT CONTROL** (Pass A: 12 curved vs 12
straight Walls in the same 3 rooms; Pass B: 40 curved vs the same 10 rooms with walls forced straight), so
curvature IS separately isolated within each pass, and Pass B even reports one within-environment
size series (compile p50 by room count, 1→10 rooms). What the controls do **not** provide is

committed, they carry no fixture identity, and they were measured under two different browsers (harvest

```text

     `cache-key-code-units`/`compiled-memory` for existing tiers. Any movement must be explained, not
     re-baselined to silence a failure. Re-recording a budget to make a red test green is forbidden.

     sample count and the warmup; never one run.
```
