Wall marks, bending, unrolling and the Card — polish pass

Revision 2 folds in the owner's review of the first pass. Revision 3 records the owner's 10 Nov amendment: the Stage pill is withdrawn and every Unroll control lives in the Card. Sections 1, 2, 4 and 5 are the approved direction. The document ends with one interaction contract (§8) and the implementation handoff (§9).

Owner amendment 3 (10 Nov 2026). The Stage pill is withdrawn. Reading name, Inside/Outside, amount, exact percentage and Put back live in one compact Unroll block on the Card, including while an Opening on the sheet is selected. §8 is the contract; the revision-2 interactive sketch below keeps its pill only as historical demonstration.

What changed in this revision.
  • The sheet's own edge is the peel. I withdraw my end knobs and the corner tab. Each free end of the unrolled sheet is the handle, drawn as an edge and nothing more.
  • Every Unroll control lives in the Card, cut from a seven-control panel to one compact session block: reading name, Inside/Outside, amount, exact entry and Put back. There is no floating pill. I withdraw "a reading's controls never appear in the Card".
  • An Opening selection keeps its own fields and adds the live Unroll block beneath them, so the reading's name, face and Put back remain reachable without a second panel.
  • Flatten-to-remove gets a snap and a preview so removal is a visible, reversible choice (§2).
Where I still push back.
  • Entry cannot be a direct gesture. On a rolled Wall the end is already the Junction handle (dragging it reshapes the Wall), and a circle has no end. So the Card-foot icon opens the reading, and direct manipulation starts from there.
  • The face switch belongs in the Card's Unroll block, not a Stage pill. When an Opening on the sheet is selected the Card remains the Opening's, with its ordinary fields, and adds the same compact Unroll context and Put back. A floating toolbar would compete with the shell; the Card is the one persistent control surface.
  • A bare edge is weak on touch, where nothing can hover. I keep one resting cue, a lift shadow under each free edge, and a 44 px hit band.
  • A straight Wall should not enter the reading. The same rule covers it (nothing to peel), so the icon stays in place and is disabled with "Already flat".
Three items need your ruling (§7). Everything else is decided here and listed as an assumption you can overturn.

2 · Bending: points that appear when relevant approved

No armed state. Shape points appear when the pointer reaches the selected Wall: solid discs are bend points, hollow discs (one per span) mean "pull here". Drag a hollow point to create a bend, a solid one to change it, the body anywhere else to move the Wall. ⌘/Ctrl-drag on the body stays as the expert path for a bend at an exact station.

Removing a bend point by dragging it flat

Removal has to be a choice, not something that happens when a small bend is released a little too low. The preview does that with four parts, all from the existing vocabulary:

1 · Pulling back Ordinary re-bend. Gold point, dashed proposal, offset tag.
2 · Snapped onto the line The point jumps to the line and turns hollow. The proposal is the Wall without it.
3 · Released The solid point has become the hollow span point it started as.

3 · Unrolling: grab the edge rewritten

What exists. Card ⋯ → Unfold Wall jumps to Flat with no fit. All control is a Card panel: range, a help sentence, a dashed Peel button, then Flat · Half · Flip · Done. The sheet has no handle. With an Opening selected the panel is not mounted at all (LayoutCardFields.svelte:59–71), so the reading has no visible exit.

The simplest model that holds for every Wall. An unrolled sheet always has exactly one pin, where it stays attached to the real Wall, and two free edges. Grab a free edge and pull: the sheet opens until that edge is under the pointer. Push it back to the Wall and it rolls up.

8 · Recommended interaction contract

This is the text I recommend adopting. It replaces the Wall row of the spatial instrument grammar and the corresponding parts of fix.md §3 and §5.

A Wall at rest

  1. A selected Wall shows its contour, its two end points and one size chip. A closed Wall shows a radius ray instead of end points. The rule is the same for straight, curved and closed Walls.
  2. Dragging the body moves the Wall rigidly. There is no move glyph; the track is drawn during the drag.
  3. The size chip toggles the length ray, the thickness arrow and, in 3D, the height stem. It is session state.
  4. During any drag only the held mark, its track and one signed Δ tag are drawn.

Bending

  1. Shape points appear while the pointer is on the selected Wall, or after a second tap on it (touch), or B (keyboard). Solid points are bend points; each span has one hollow point. They leave with the pointer unless a point is active.
  2. Dragging a hollow point creates a bend. Dragging a solid point changes it. ⌘/Ctrl-drag on the body bends at that exact station. Nothing arms a mode, on the Stage or in the Card.
  3. A bend point dragged to within the snap distance of the line between its neighbours snaps onto it. The point turns hollow, the proposal shows the Wall without it and the tag reads Remove bend point. Release removes it as one history entry; Undo restores it where it was before the drag.
  4. The removal zone is left at a larger distance than it is entered. A point must have been outside the zone during the drag before it can be removed; a new point released before that makes no bend and no history entry.
  5. Delete on the active point and ✕ on the Card's bend-point row also remove it. The row appears only while a point is active and holds its exact X and Z.

Unrolling

  1. Entry. Every Wall Card has an Unroll icon at its foot. It is disabled with "Already flat" on a straight Wall. Pressing it rolls the sheet out to Flat.
  2. Framing. Entry keeps the Camera's orientation and makes one pan/zoom fit to the as-built Wall plus the Flat sheet, inside the Stage safe area. Nothing else in the reading moves the Camera.
  3. Shape of the sheet. A sheet has one pin and two free edges. On an open Wall the free edges are its ends. On a closed Wall they are the two sides of a cut opposite the pin; the cut never falls inside an Opening.
  4. Peel. Dragging a free edge sets the unroll amount so the edge follows the pointer, with magnetic stops at Half and Flat. Returned to the Wall, the tag reads Release to put back and release ends the reading. Peeling writes no history.
  5. Edge affordance. A free edge is drawn at contour weight with a lift shadow. On hover or press it turns leaf and shows its track home. Its hit band is at least 44 px on touch.
  6. Card Unroll block. While a Wall is unrolled its Card shows one compact block: reading name, Inside/Outside, a range with its percentage and Half and Flat as stops, an exact percentage field, and Put back. The amount appears nowhere else. There is no Stage pill or replacement toolbar.
  7. Opening selection. Clicking an Opening on the sheet selects it; the Card keeps the Opening's own fields and adds the same compact Unroll block beneath them. Put back therefore stays reachable without a second panel.
  8. Face. The sheet opens showing the face toward the Camera (in Plan, the Room side). The Card's Inside/Outside switch turns the sheet half a turn about the pin's vertical. Faces are named by what they bound. The Camera does not move.
  9. Pin. The pin starts at the selected Opening's centre, else at the Wall station under the view centre. Dragging it along the as-built footprint rolls the sheet round the Wall. It is display state.
  10. Selection. Clicking an Opening on the sheet selects it and the Card keeps that Opening's fields plus the compact Unroll block. Clicking the sheet elsewhere selects the Wall. Opening handles on the sheet are the as-built kit placed through the development map, and every edit reaches the canonical planner in canonical stations. Edits to the Wall's own shape are refused while it is unrolled.
  11. Exit. Put back, Esc with no gesture held, or rolling an edge home. The Camera returns to its entry pose only if the creator has not navigated since entry.
  12. Words. Unroll, Put back, pin, bend point. Unfold, Refold, Flip, Done and Peel are not UI copy.
